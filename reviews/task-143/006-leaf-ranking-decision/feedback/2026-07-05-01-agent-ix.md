## Findings

### Finding 1 (MEDIUM, decision boundary) — the half-nprobe gate undersells leaf-only

The decision keeps leaf-only default-off because the aggressive "recall at
nprobe X ≥ recall at nprobe 2X before" gate is missed at 50k/100k. That gate is
a *frontier-re-anchoring* bar, not a *safe-to-enable* bar. On the metric this
task exists to fix (distinct recall — a localized routing recall defect),
leaf-only is effectively **strictly dominating**: recall-positive at every
nprobe and every scale, latency-neutral-to-better, with a first-principles
justification (removing injected noise from an exactly-scored leaf term). A
change that never regresses anything and lifts 100k recall by up to +0.046 is a
reasonable default-on candidate on its own merits. I do not block the
conservative call, but the packet should either (a) recommend flipping
leaf-only default-on, or (b) state explicitly why strict Pareto dominance is
insufficient and only the 2×-nprobe gate governs promotion. As written a reader
could reasonably conclude leaf-only should already ship.

### Finding 2 (LOW, config coverage) — dominance is only shown on the 2-level exact-leaf grid

The only real caveat *for* keeping leaf-only behind a GUC is that the A/B
covers a single index shape (2-level hierarchy, all 1024 leaf centroids scored
exactly in f32, `recursive_fanout=8`, b0). `path_score` was dropped from
ranking but retained for descent/tie-break; in deeper hierarchies or with
approximate leaf scoring the path term may carry signal. This is a legitimate
reason to gate default-on until broader shapes are covered (Task 146
frontier/shape work) — but it is *not* the reason the packet gives (it cites
the half-nprobe gate). Recommend the packet name config coverage as the actual
gating rationale; it is the stronger argument.

### Finding 3 (LOW, attribution completeness) — both-levers-on cell unmeasured

Overfetch always reranks the cushion by pure leaf score (`leaf_route_leaf_score_cmp`),
so `leaf_score_only=on` + `α>1` is a distinct configuration (leaf-ranked
top-nprobe drawn from a wider deduped pool). It is never benched. Not required
for this promote/negative decision, but if Task 146 revisits promotion it
should include the combined cell rather than assume it equals leaf-only.

### Finding 4 (INFO) — funnel diagnostics hardcode AccumulatedPathScore

`collect_recursive_routing_level_diagnostics_with_budget` and
`route_recursive_routing_objects_to_leaf_routes_with_policy` pass
`SpireLeafRouteRanking::AccumulatedPathScore` unconditionally. The recall/latency
numbers come from the real force-index scan (which threads
`scan_plan.leaf_route_ranking` correctly), so the headline evidence is
unaffected; only the `*-funnel.jsonl` diagnostic stream may not reflect
leaf-only mode. Cosmetic for this decision; worth aligning if the funnel is ever
used to argue a leaf-only containment number directly.
