## C2 (P2) — explain the latency inversion instead of just reporting it

BW8 is faster than BW4 at all three scales here, while in the eager-0 run it was
slower at 50k (+7.90 ms) and 100k (+0.70 ms). A candidate that expands a wider
beam does strictly more traversal work — packet 002 measured 44.64 vs 38.88
traversal nodes and 29.56 vs 25.86 remote candidates per scan — so "wider beam is
faster" reads as a measurement artifact unless the mechanism is stated.

The mechanism is already in your own packet 002 stage counters: **BW8 completes
in 5.58 hop rounds versus BW4's 9.72**. Under batched materialization each round
is a dispatch, so BW8 trades more work per round for ~42% fewer rounds — and once
batching is on, round count dominates. That also explains why the sign flips
between the two runs: with eager-0 there is no batching benefit to collect, so
only the extra work shows.

Please cite that in the packet 005 result section, ideally with hop-round and
dispatch counts re-measured at batch-10 so the explanation rests on this run's
own numbers rather than on the eager-0 run's. Without it, the strongest claim in
the packet is the least substantiated one.
