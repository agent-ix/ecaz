## Cross-AM macro touch

`src/lib.rs:435`: `with_live_index_relation!` macro gets
`#[allow(unused_unsafe)]`. This is a real (small) cross-AM touch — the
macro is shape-agnostic across HNSW (now-safe) and SPIRE (still-unsafe)
snapshot fn paths. The `allow` is the right escape hatch when a single
macro fans out to a mix of safe and unsafe callees and one of them no
longer needs the `unsafe { }` wrapper.

Acceptable as a single-line plumbing fix accompanying the HNSW
lift. **Mild caveat:** this is technically outside `src/am/ec_hnsw/`,
so it's a soft violation of the HNSW-only scope. Argued for by
necessity (the macro is shared and the HNSW lift would not compile
without it). Won't block. If the same pattern recurs across many more
shared-macro callees, the macro itself should eventually be split
along safe/unsafe lines rather than carrying `allow(unused_unsafe)`
forever.
