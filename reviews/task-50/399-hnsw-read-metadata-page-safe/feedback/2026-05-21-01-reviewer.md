## Soundness note (non-blocking)

`pub(crate) fn read_metadata_page(index_relation: pg_sys::Relation)` is the
soft-contract pattern: a safe function that accepts a raw `*mut RelationData`
and dereferences it through `read_main_buffer`, which itself is a safe `fn`
making the same assumption (see `shared.rs:64`). This is consistent with the
established `read_main_buffer` precedent the request cites, so it is the right
move *for this slice*. It is **not** the strongest form of Task 50 §Techniques
#1 (Encapsulate at FFI boundary) / #2 (Lift invariants into references) — the
strongest form would take `IndexRelationGuard` or `NonNull<RelationData>` and
push the "live relation" invariant into the parameter type.

Do not retrofit that here — it would balloon the slice. Capture as a separate
HNSW slice candidate: a follow-up that retypes both `read_main_buffer` and
`read_metadata_page` (and probably a small audit of similar safe helpers in
`shared.rs`) to take a guarded handle. That slice would also unblock more
caller-side safety lifts in `count_element_tuples`,
`index_admin_snapshot`, and `index_cost_snapshot`, which currently remain
`pub(super)/pub(crate) unsafe fn` only because they accept a raw
`pg_sys::Relation` argument they then thread into other code.
