## The remaining gate — HNSW bench window

The packet correctly names this as the outstanding §Exit-Criteria
item. Its risk-attenuation argument is reasonable:

> The 47-packet rotation made no allocation-shape changes, no
> scoring-math changes, no WAL-ordering changes, and no
> payload-byte changes. Every slice was a pure signature flip,
> caller-wrapper cleanup, or typed-wrapper introduction.

This is **mostly true**, with two specific exceptions worth
flagging for the bench-runner's attention:

1. **406's dispatcher restructure** in
   `score_grouped_candidate_context` hoisted both predicate
   booleans (`exact_layer`, `binary_score`) ahead of the branch
   selector. Semantically identical (predicates are
   side-effect-free), but the branch shape did change.
2. **408's call-site reorder** in
   `refine_grouped_frontier_head_exact` moved the
   `opaque_ptr = opaque as *mut TqScanOpaque` binding **after** the
   `cached_graph_element` call. This is a borrow-flow change, not
   pure signature.
3. **415's borrow-tightening** in
   `cached_scan_successor_candidates_for_layer` replaced the
   long-lived `opaque_ref` binding with tight inner-loop
   re-acquisitions. The CPU sees the same call sequence, but the
   borrow scopes are now narrower; LLVM may inline differently.

If the bench window shows a regression, those three are the first
places to look. Otherwise the rotation's "no observable behavior
change" claim is sound.

**Recommendation**: run the bench window before this rotation is
filed as fully complete. The packet's queueing of it for the
"next bench window" is the right disposition; flag it as
**§Exit Criteria gate, not optional follow-up**.
