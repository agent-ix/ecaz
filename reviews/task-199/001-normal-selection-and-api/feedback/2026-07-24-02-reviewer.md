  restatement of it: `blocking_build_mutation_fenced ... waiting_lock=RowExclusiveLock

  "remove the redundant guard" cleanup, and the comment added in `routine.rs`
  says why it exists.

  local-demotion path outright is better than making it non-fatal: it removes a
  catalog write from a user `SELECT` unconditionally, so `READ ONLY`
  transactions and standbys cannot be affected by it at all.

  statement trigger → `CacheInvalidateRelcacheByRelid` → global relcache
  callback) rather than inventing a new invalidation channel, and it is checked
  *before* the SPI work in all three call sites rather than inside them.
