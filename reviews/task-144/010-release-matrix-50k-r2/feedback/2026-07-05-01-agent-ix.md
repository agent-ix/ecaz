## Headline: the 10k AC does NOT hold at 50k — nothing clears ≥0.99 @ ≤5%

At 10k, closure-ε at low nprobe cleared ≥0.99 distinct_recall@10 at ~2.6–3.0%
candidate-row-instances scanned. **That collapses at 50k.** Recomputed from raw
data, the global set of cells reaching `distinct_recall@10 ≥ 0.99`, sorted by the
AC metric `candidate_row_instances_percent` (= candidate_sum / queries / 50000 ×
100, candidate phase):

```
80.9118     closure_e050_b8-ratio200   96      0.9900   491.704 ms    37.820 ms
```

- **No 50k row satisfies ≥0.99 @ ≤5% candidate.** The cheapest 0.99 point,
  `fixed_b2-adaptive @ np96`, scans **35.68%** — 7× the ≤5% budget (and 20.69%
  even on the softer ready-row metric, still 4×). Verified against raw
  `candidate_sum=3568343 → 3568343/200/50000·100 = 35.6834`.
- **Best recall reachable at ≤5% candidate is only ~0.90** (`fixed_b2-ratio125 @
  np16` = 0.9020 @ 4.52%; every single-* cell tops out at 0.876 @ ~4.1%). There
  is no operating point anywhere near the AC.
- **Every 0.99 row requires nprobe 96** (the top of the SPIRE sweep). At 50k the
  index simply needs to scan far more of the corpus to recover the last percent
  of recall.

This is a **major finding**, stated plainly: the AC that held at 10k **fails at
50k**, and it fails by a wide margin (7×), not a near-miss.

## closure-ε is NOT the winning lever at 50k — it's dominated by plain fixed_b2

At 10k, closure-ε at np16 was the lever that cleared the AC and ratio pruning
earned no operating point. **At 50k, closure-ε does not scale.** The cheapest
0.99 point is the *plain 3× replication baseline* (`fixed_b2`, no closure), and
closure is strictly **worse**: closure_e025 needs 58.8%, closure_e050 needs
80.9–86.1%. Closure buys low-nprobe recall lift, but at 50k that lift is not
enough to reach 0.99 below np96, and at np96 closure's larger candidate sets just
cost more scan and storage for the same recall. On this 50k evidence closure
assignment is **not promotable** — it is dominated by fixed_b2 at the only 0.99
points that exist.

## Ratio pruning STILL earns no operating point (same disposition as 10k)

The np96 recall-by-ratio table (verified from harness tables):

```
single             0.8145  0.9515  0.9515  0.9515  0.9515  0.9555
fixed_b2           0.9450  0.9850  0.9850  0.9850  0.9850  0.9900

```

At np96, **ratio ≥2.0 gives recall identical to fixed** (r2.0 = r4.0 = r8.0 =
fixed, exact) — i.e. at these ratios the distance filter never bites, it's "no
pruning." Only r1.25 changes the candidate set, and it does so by **collapsing
recall** (single 0.9515→0.8145, closure_e050 0.99→0.961). So the dial is
technically live only at the tight end where it destroys recall; there is no
ratio that both prunes and holds ≥0.99. This reproduces the 10k conclusion — if
anything the dial is *more* inert at 50k (r2/r4/r8 are bit-identical at np96).
**Retire ratio pruning as a headline lever** on the 10k+50k evidence.
