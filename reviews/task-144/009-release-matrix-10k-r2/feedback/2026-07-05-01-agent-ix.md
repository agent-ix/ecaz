## Crux #1 — surrogate: VARIES with the ratio, NOT the packet-004 flat collapse

This is the headline. **Recall now varies sensibly and monotonically with the
ratio dial** — the packet-004 "ratio has no effect, flat vs nprobe" collapse is
*not* what 009 shows. Verified from raw `distinct_recall@k` at nprobe 96:

```
config           r1.25    r2.0     r4.0     r8.0
single           0.7635   0.9575   0.9895   0.9935
closure_e010_b8  0.7680   0.9585   0.9900   0.9940
closure_e025_b8  0.7795   0.9620   0.9915   0.9950
closure_e050_b8  0.8010   0.9640   0.9925   0.9950
fixed_b2         0.9150   0.9850   0.9930   0.9955
```

The ratio is a **real multiplicative dial**: if the floored-IP surrogate
`(1-score).max(0)` had degenerated to all-zeros, `ratio × best` would be `0` at
*every* ratio and all four columns would be identical — they are not. So the
mechanism responds to the knob; the surrogate is working, not collapsed.

**But the operational conclusion for ratio pruning is negative.** The behavior is
pincered:
- **Tight ratios collapse.** `r1.25` is still dead — recall pinned at the floor
  (single `0.7585→0.7635` across the *entire* nprobe 8→96 sweep, invariant to
  nprobe). `r2.0` recovers to ~0.94–0.96 but never reaches 0.99 at any nprobe.
- **Loose ratios barely prune.** `r4.0`/`r8.0` recover recall only by keeping so
  many routes that at nprobe 96 they scan **7–11%** (single-r4.0 6.95%, r8.0
  10.09%; closure_e050-r4.0 10.86% — all recomputed as
  `candidate_sum/(corpus_rows·queries)·100`), blowing the ≤5% AC. `r8.0` is
  essentially "no pruning" — its recall tracks `fixed`/`adaptive`.

There is **no ratio where pruning both bites (cuts scan below the closure-ε +
low-nprobe frontier) and holds recall ≥0.99.** So the ratio-sweep route does not
*fail* the way 004 implied (the dial is live), but it also does **not earn a new
operating point.** The norm-robust surrogate is only worth building if the goal
is aggressive pruning at *tight* ratios that still holds recall — it is **not
required** to clear the 10k AC, because closure-ε already does.

## Crux #4 — attribution (mechanisms ARE isolable, not just stacked)

The matrix crosses {single, fixed_b2, closure_e010/025/050} × {fixed, adaptive,
ratio125/200/400/800}, so `single×fixed` isolates the baseline, `closure×fixed`
isolates closure-ε, `single×ratio` isolates ratio pruning, and
`closure×ratio` is the combination. Reading the corners at np16:

- **closure-ε is the lever.** `single-fixed@np16` = 0.9880 (below AC);
  `closure_e050_b8-fixed@np16` = 0.9915 (clears AC). Closure-ε lifts low-nprobe
  recall over the 0.99 line — that is what buys the low-scan operating point.
- **ratio pruning adds only a marginal trim.** On top of closure_e050 at np16,
  `ratio400` moves candidate 2.96%→2.57% (~13% fewer instances) but drops recall
  0.9915→0.9900 (to the floor). Not a distinct win over just running fixed.
- **adaptive** ≈ fixed at the AC points (`single-adaptive@np32` 0.9925 @ 3.70%).

Attribution is clean and the coder's own reading ("clearest AC points are
closure-based") is correct.
