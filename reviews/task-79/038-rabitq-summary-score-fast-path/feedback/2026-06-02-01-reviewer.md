# Task 79 packet 038 — summary-score fast path

## Decision

**Accept as small real optimization to land.** ~1ms p50 improvement
at global736 (48.85 → 47.91ms) by precomputing scoring context
once per pass instead of per-representative. The improvement is
small but production-safe, and the change is worth keeping
regardless of Task 79 closure.

The remaining gap (2.91ms over the 45ms gate) is now ~6.5% — the
closest yet, but not closure.

## Useful attribution finding

The packet's interpretation is correct: "scorer dispatch/validation
overhead is not the remaining dominant cost." 1ms of the ~4ms
overhead was dispatch; the other ~3ms is **actual scoring kernel
CPU**. That means further optimization needs to attack the
quantizer inner loop, not the framework around it.

That bounds the remaining technical paths:
- SIMD/cache-aware multi-rep RaBitQ scoring (technically
  significant work)
- Lookup-table tricks for the bit-popcount step
- Possibly batched query×N-summary scoring across a routed leaf

## Why this is worth keeping even without closure

- 1ms × N queries/sec is a real production saving at high QPS
- It's safe (no recall/correctness impact, validated)
- It's a small focused change with tests
- It improves the operational profile of the best-known
  shipping recipe

Recommend merging the fast-path change regardless of whether
Task 79 closes formally.
