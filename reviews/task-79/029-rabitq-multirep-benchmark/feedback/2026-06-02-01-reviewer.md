## Cost model worked out

Earlier I estimated multi-rep at k=4 would add ~2.65ms to p50.
At k=2 (this packet's choice), the estimate was ~1.3ms. Actual
result at g640/w=0.25: 44.85ms p50 vs prior block32 baseline
~47.6ms. So multi-rep at k=2 *reduced* p50 by ~2.8ms because the
smaller block budget (640 vs 896) outweighed the +1.3ms multi-rep
overhead. Net latency win.

## What needs to happen for closure

We need ~11 more exact targets to clear the recall gate at
g640 (per packet 030 diagnostic). That's 0.55pp = 11/2000. Very
tight gap.

Three plausible paths from here:

1. **k=3 or k=4 representatives** — more discrimination at higher
   summary CPU. At k=4, p50 would add ~1.3ms more → ~46ms, just
   over the gate. Marginal viability.
2. **Per-leaf score calibration** — keep k=2 but learn a per-leaf
   normalization so summaries from different leaves are
   comparable. Cheap (build-time only). Potentially recovers
   near-cap misses.
3. **Calibrate the radius blend weight** — w=0.25 was a guess.
   The optimal weight may differ at k=2 vs single-rep. Sweep
   0.1-0.5 at k=2 to find the maximum.

(3) is the cheapest first try and might be enough — 11 misses
recovery on the radius-weight axis is plausible. (2) is next.
(1) is the heaviest.
