## Why per-leaf quota fails

The packet 014/015 finding established that global allocation
naturally directs budget toward high-information leaves and away
from low-information ones. Per-leaf quota fights that distribution
— forcing each routed leaf to contribute equally, regardless of
its summary quality.

In a fixture where ~75% of routed leaves contain useful winners,
forcing each leaf to contribute 8 blocks means:
- High-quality leaves get *under*-allocated (8 < what global would
  give them)
- Low-quality leaves get *over*-allocated (8 > what global would
  give them)
- The net candidate count is similar but information content is
  worse

That's the recall collapse mechanism.

## What this rules out

Per-leaf allocation discipline is now closed at both the
single-rep (010-013) and multi-rep (034) layers. The conclusion is
robust: **global allocation is the correct strategy for SPIRE
block selection at all summary granularities tested.**
