# Task 79 packet 028 — clustered block16 radius-weight benchmark

## Decision

**Accept as block-size-sweep closure.** Block16 confirms the
geometric ceiling: shrinking blocks further than block32 brings
candidates *under* the gate for the first time, but recall stalls
at the same ~0.989-0.990 ceiling and p50 worsens from the extra
summary scoring overhead. The block size axis is now bounded.

Coder has already pivoted to multi-rep (packet 029 in flight) —
correctly.

## Block size sweep summary

| block | best row | candidates | p50 | recall |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 64 | g416/w0.25 (025) | 5.20M ✓ | 45.8ms ✓ | 0.9835 ✗ |
| 32 | g896/w0.25 (027) | 5.67M ✗ | 47.6ms ✗ | 0.9900 ✗ |
| 16 | g1536/w0.25 (028) | **4.89M ✓** | 48.6ms ✗ | 0.9890 ✗ |

Three observations:

1. **Candidates: block16 finally clears the gate** (4.89M < 5.2M).
   Confirmation that the candidate surface can be reduced enough
   with finer blocks at the right cap.
2. **p50: increasingly worse** with smaller blocks. block16 has
   4× more summaries than block64 = more summary scoring cost.
   That's the per-summary cost we discussed earlier, now visible.
3. **Recall: ceiling at ~0.989-0.990**. Block16 gives 0.9890
   (slightly *worse* than block32's 0.9900 at the higher-recall
   row). Smaller blocks aren't lifting recall — they're moving
   it sideways.

The recall ceiling is structural to single-scalar-per-block
summaries. The block-size axis is exhausted at the recall side.

## Independent confirmation of packet 026's diagnostic

Packet 026's rank-distribution finding predicted exactly this:
"the misses are spread across ranks 400-1000+, not clustered at
the cap threshold." Finer blocks effectively double-or-quadruple
the rank scale (block16 has 4× more rank positions than block64),
but the *fraction* of winners at each rank tier doesn't change.
So block16/g1536 reaches roughly the same recall as
block32/g768 — same ceiling, same shape.

## Verdict

Block-size sweep is complete. The three packets (025/027/028)
form a clean Pareto curve where you can pick:

- **Best p50**: block64 g416/w0.25 (45.8ms, 0.9835 recall)
- **Best recall under cap**: block32 g896/w0.25 (5.67M cands,
  but slightly over gate, 0.9900 recall)
- **Best candidate compliance**: block16 g1536/w0.25 (4.89M
  cands ✓, but 48.6ms p50, 0.9890 recall)

None close all three Task 79 gates simultaneously, by ~1pp on
recall in every case.

Coder's pivot to multi-rep (packet 029) is the right move. The
block-size sweep was exhaustive and bounded by the structural
constraint identified in packet 026. Multi-rep directly attacks
the summary-score-discrimination wall that block size cannot.
