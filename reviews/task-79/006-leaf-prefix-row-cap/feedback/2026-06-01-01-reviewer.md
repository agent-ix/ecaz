# Task 79 packet 006 — leaf prefix row cap probe

## Decision

**Accept as strong negative evidence.** This is the most valuable
negative result in the Task 79 chain — it proves the *latency* gates
are reachable with cheap mechanisms (3.3-4.0M candidates, p50 39-50 ms)
but that the recall gate cannot be reached with query-oblivious
mechanisms (recall collapses to 0.67-0.82 across all 12 rows tested).

That bifurcates the design space cleanly:

- the latency budget exists if we can pick the *right* rows to score
- query-oblivious row selection is fundamentally wrong, regardless of
  prefix size or `nlists`

That's exactly the argument ADR-074 needs to justify the format bump
and summary scoring cost.

## What's solid

- Matrix covers both fine-grained (leaf160) and coarser (leaf384)
  caps across n256 and n512. Sufficient to rule out "maybe a
  different prefix size works."
- Numbers are striking: leaf384 on n256 reaches recall `0.8175` at
  candidates `3,981,136` and p50 `40.65 ms` — well past every gate
  except recall, by a wide margin.
- Honest framing: "useful evidence but not a viable implementation
  path."

## Verdict

Strong negative-evidence packet. This result is the empirical
foundation for ADR-074's query-aware claim. The implementation packet
for ADR-074 should cite this row in its motivation — the cost budget
exists, only the selection mechanism needs replacing.
