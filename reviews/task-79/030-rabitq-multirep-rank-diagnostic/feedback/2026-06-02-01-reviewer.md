# Task 79 packet 030 — multi-rep rank diagnostic

## Decision

**Accept as critical localization data.** The diagnostic confirms
the multi-rep failure mode is now extremely narrow — only **11
missed targets at cap640** separate us from recall gate closure.

That's the smallest residual gap in the entire 30-packet
sequence.

## Direct comparison to packet 026

| diagnostic | cap | targets selected | targets missed |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| single-rep block64 (026) | 384 | 1,938 | 62 |
| **multi-rep block32 (030)** | **640** | **1,974** | **26** |
| multi-rep block32 (030) | 768 | 1,981 | 19 |

Multi-rep at cap640 misses 26 targets, vs single-rep at cap384
missing 62 targets. The miss count dropped 58% even as we shrank
the candidate budget (4.05M vs 5.20M cands).

To hit the 0.9925 gate, we need ≤15 misses. At cap640 we're at
26 misses — **11 misses away**. At cap768 we're at 19 misses — 4
misses away, but candidates pass while p50 fails.

## What this implies

The packet 026 diagnostic had p99=588, max=1099. If the multi-rep
rank distribution is materially tighter (which the recall
improvement implies), the 11 misses at cap640 are likely
*clustered* in the rank range 641-700 rather than spread to
rank 1000+.

If true, a small cap bump (say cap680-720) at the same multi-rep
config would likely close the recall gate. But cap bumps cost
candidates and p50:

| cap | candidates est. | p50 est. | recall est. | gates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 640 (measured) | 4.05M | 44.85 ms | 0.9870 | recall ✗ |
| ~680 (est) | ~4.30M | ~45.5 ms | ~0.989 | maybe p50 ✗ |
| ~720 (est) | ~4.55M | ~46.2 ms | ~0.991 | p50 ✗ |
| 768 (measured) | 4.86M | 47.6 ms | 0.9905 | p50 ✗ |

So pure cap-bumping at multi-rep+w=0.25 likely fails on p50 by
1-2ms. We need ranking improvement, not budget.

## What the diagnostic doesn't tell us yet

The packet doesn't include the full rank distribution (p50, p90,
p99) for the multi-rep selector. With single-rep packet 026 we
saw p99=588, max=1099. If multi-rep p99 is ~750-800, that
confirms 11 misses are clustered just past cap640. If p99 is
~600-650, the misses are clustered at exactly cap640 boundary
and the right fix is a per-leaf score recalibration.

Worth adding the percentile distribution to a follow-up
diagnostic before committing to k=3 or other format work. That
data point determines whether (a) cap bump fails because of
spread distribution or (b) cap bump might just work because the
spread is tight.

## Verdict

Strongest residual-gap localization in the sequence. Confirms
multi-rep moved the wall — we now need to close 11 misses, not
57. The next selector-side slice should target those 11 misses
specifically.
