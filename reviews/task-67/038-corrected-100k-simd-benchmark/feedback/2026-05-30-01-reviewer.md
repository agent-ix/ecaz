Across `100k-{scalar,auto}/sidecar-*.log`, `suite-run.log`, `suite-manifest.json`, and `results.jsonl`, grep for `backend|simd|avx|fallback|ECAZ_SIMD` returns no in-band attestation. ### F2 (medium) — bits=1 vs bits=8 SQL-headline question at 100k is not answered

Per 027 the bits=8 SQL headline at 10k was only 1.09x because the scorer is ~1% of wall time. This packet's measurements confirm the same fractional structure at 100k: `sidecar_score_p50` (~0.02-0.11 ms) is roughly 0.1-0.8% of `total_bound_p50` (11-24 ms). The 5-6x kernel speedup compresses to 1.20-1.27x end-to-end — same gate-met outcome as 10k, just at a larger candidate-SQL absolute. So:

- The 100k corpus does not shift the gate-met outcome for the rabitq8 sidecar at quant_bits=1.
- The bits=1 vs bits=8 SQL-headline comparison itself is not present in this packet — every result row is `quant_bits=1` with a rabitq8 sidecar. To close out 027 at 100k, a sibling packet should run a quant_bits=8 SQL-rerank lane (no sidecar) at 100k on the same host and compare `total_bound_p50` directly. This packet does not block 027 closure but it does not advance it either.
