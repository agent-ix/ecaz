## Contract review (`f305ae227`)

FR-079's new normative bullets (executor-driven windows of 10, requests
bounded by the proven prefix, no re-request after deepening, qual deepening

**Finding (P2, spec precision): the NFR-019 no-qual bound ignores
tombstone/invisible-row skips.** The table asserts unqualified payload reads
`≤ min(D, 10 × ceil(k/10))` and says "any breach fails the run". But a
`RemoteSkipped` tombstone or a snapshot-invisible local row inside the ranked
prefix yields no executor row, so an unqualified LIMIT-k scan advances into
additional windows exactly as a qual rejection would. With t skipped slots
the true bound is `min(D, 10 × ceil((k + t)/10))`. The measured corpora have
zero tombstones, so nothing observed breached, but as written a
tombstone-bearing corpus could fail a "no-qual" assertion (or worse, teach
someone the wrong invariant). Fix by qualifying the no-qual row ("in the
absence of tombstone/visibility skips") or by folding skips into the
qual-driven `D` case, in both NFR-019 and the packet-001 statement. The
`≤ D` ceiling and the total-read bounds are unaffected.
