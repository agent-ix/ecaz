## Required before status flip (documentation, not a re-run)

### The defer must rest on "no win on real data," not on the synthetic −7.3pp

Packet 008 states the synthetic cross-corpus regression "is the closeout reason,"
and 007 leans the gate on it as the stop-condition trigger. The synthetic fixture
is the **weakest** evidence in the packet and should be corroborating, not primary:

1. It is a uniform **unit-sphere** distribution (seed 8906), not a real
   non-DBPedia embedder. The task's load-bearing cross-corpus question — does the
   per-coordinate calibration generalize across *embedder families* (OpenAI /
   Cohere / multilingual / image) — is therefore still literally untested.
2. Unit-sphere data is adversarial to this calibration *by construction*: TQ+
   fits per-dim shift/scale from the 5th/95th percentiles of the SRHT-rotated
   sample; on i.i.d. spherical coordinates there is little per-dim signal to
   exploit, so a regression there is the expected degenerate case and overstates
   real-world risk. Recall is already 0.38–0.86 (a low-SNR retrieval regime),
   which further inflates the apparent deltas.
3. Resting a defer on the single most adversarial fixture invites a "that's not
   representative" reopen later.

The good news: **the defer stands on its own without the synthetic number.** On
the only *real* embedder tested (DBPedia), TQ+ shows no durable win —
10k −0.50 pp, 50k +0.30 pp, 100k −0.60 pp (net neutral/negative, no consistent
direction), +0.30 pp on projected QJL, neutral-to-slightly-worse storage, and no
latency case until a SIMD scorer lands. For a new variant that adds calibration
complexity, a metadata-version bump, per-row storage, and a not-yet-written
kernel, **"no reliable recall win on real data" is sufficient grounds not to
promote.** That is the honest, defensible primary reason.

So the only change I'm asking for: in 008 (and the eventual task-status note),
lead the closeout reason with the DBPedia/QJL no-win, cite the synthetic
regression as *corroborating* and as the formal stop-condition trigger, and
record prominently — not buried in "Follow-Up Guidance" — that **no real
non-DBPedia embedder was measured, so any future promotion attempt must clear a
real cross-corpus recall pass** (and the comparable-scorer latency pass). You
already say this in follow-up; just elevate it into the decision itself.
