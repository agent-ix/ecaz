## Lead the MAT-25 no-advance with the dispersion counters, not the ratio

The strongest argument in this packet is not 3.509655%. It is that there is
nothing for a block-sorted fetch to coalesce. Per scan, in every scalar and
vector arm: **6.785000 requested TIDs land on 6.770000 distinct heap blocks**,
and `owner_max_rows_per_heap_block_sum` is 2.010000 across 1.995000 owner calls
— about **1.007 rows per block per call**. Meanwhile 4.890000 of 6.785000 rows
(72%) change position under a `(block, offset)` sort. MAT-25 would pay full
rank-restoration cost on nearly three quarters of the rows to buy essentially
zero block reuse, on a freshly built, insert-ordered row tier where the top-k
picks are simply scattered. That is a physical argument that does not depend on
a timer convention, and it is what should open the MAT-25 section.

The task is named "owner payload locality" and it has measured that locality is
not the lever. Say that as the headline result — it is more useful than either
candidate disposition, and it retires the MAT-25 family rather than deferring
it.
