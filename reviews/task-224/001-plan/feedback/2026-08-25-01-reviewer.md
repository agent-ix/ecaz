## The dependency argument is correct, and the data now proves it

I want to be clear that I accept the core premise, because it could have looked
like a way around the STOP I had just accepted. It is not. Task 223's ceiling
was measured on the **id-only** projection — Task 222's production mask, 11.60 ms
warm mean, 0.514999 ms bucket. A vector-bearing or externally toasted projection
is a different workload with a different denominator, and the plan is right that
it "must be measured rather than inferred from the id-only result."

The uncommitted packet-002 run settles it. Reading
`reviews/task-224/002-locality-attribution/artifacts/run/results.jsonl`, per-arm
100k warm means and `materialize_owner_payload_sql_work`:

| arm | warm mean | payload_sql (summed) | 5% gate | 1 ms gate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| id-only | 11.80 | 0.5138 | 0.590 — fail | fail |
| narrow-scalar | 12.10 | 0.5909 | 0.605 — fail | fail |
| vector-bearing | 28.20 | 7.8292 | 1.410 — **pass** | **pass** |
| toasted | 46.10 | 2.1215 | 2.305 — fail | **pass** |

Two things worth calling out before you write packet 002:

**The id-only arm reproduces Task 223 to within 0.2%** (0.5138 here vs 0.514999
there, 11.80 vs 11.60 warm). That is a real independent replication of the
number my Task 223 acceptance rested on, on a fresh generation. It is the
strongest evidence in this packet and the request should say so.

**The task's own hypothesis looks wrong, and that is the finding.** For
vector-bearing, `materialize_owner_binary_send_work` is 6.9680 ms of the 7.7996
ms `materialize_owner_payload_spi_work` — and since only `SendFunctionCall` is
charged to `binary_send_ns` (per the comment at `generation_read.rs:2977`), the
heap-access/detoast residual is roughly **0.83 ms**. Under the plan's own
"independently addressable bucket" rule, MAT-25 (heap-block/TID reorder) has a
sub-1 ms ceiling *in the worst arm measured*, while MAT-26 (binary send) clears
the gate by ~5×. A task named "owner payload locality" has measured that
locality is not the lever; send-function cost is. Say that plainly in packet
002 rather than letting the tie-break rule reach it silently — it is a more
useful result than either candidate.

That also means item 1 above is not academic: on the critical-path reading,
vector-bearing's addressable ceiling is bounded by
`materialize_owner_endpoint_critical` = 5.1490 ms, not 7.8292 ms. Still a
decisive pass, but a 34% smaller prize, and the toasted arm drops from 4.60% to
3.88% of warm mean.
