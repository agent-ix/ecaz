### The falsification — why the transport hypothesis was wrong

My steer across 007/008 was: latency is ~140 ms transport-dominated, so fewer/larger
leaves should mean fewer remote round-trips / less candidate transport and cut the
floor. The counters refute this directly — off vs on, they are **identical**:

| | n1024 | n128 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `dispatch_sum` (np16+) | 600 | 600 |
| `remote_heap_candidate_sum` (np32+) | 30000 | 30000 |
| `socket_open_sum` | 0 | 0 |
| `connection_pool_hit_sum` | 600 | 600 |

Round-trips (`dispatch_sum=600`) are fixed by node topology × query count, not leaf
count. Transported candidates saturate at the **same 30000** heap frontier in both
geometries. The leaf-count reduction only shrank the per-node leaf-routing component
(`leaf_count_p50` 1.38 ms → 0.11 ms) — a ~1 ms move against a ~140 ms floor, i.e.
noise. The transport-dominated cost lives in dispatch count and frontier size;
**leaf geometry touches neither.** So the last promising unmeasured lever is now
measured, and it does not move the floor. This is a clean, honest result — I flag my
own prior hypothesis as falsified, not the coder's work.

## 3. Recall / storage / build trades

- **Recall:** drops badly and pathologically (peaks 0.848@np16, collapses to
  0.784@np96). Outputs are stressed — the cells diverge by 0.15 recall, not
  byte-identical. Metric is authoritative (`recall@k == distinct_recall@k`).
- **Block pruning:** engages far *more* in n128 (summed over 3 remotes, np96 fixed):
  blocks available 126,184 (n1024) → **964,963** (n128); skipped 49,387 → **888,163**.
  But this is work created, not saved: larger leaves mean vastly more block summaries
  to score even after pruning.
- **Compute goes UP, not down:** `candidate_score_nanos_sum` at np96 fixed ≈ **622M**
  (n1024) vs **≈1.376B** (n128) — n128 does ~2.2x more scoring work per query. That
  is the opposite of an economy win; it is exactly why the small per-node latency
  savings from fewer leaves are cancelled.
- **Storage:** modest real improvement, 111.0 → 98.5 MiB SPIRE index (~11%, ~12.5
  MiB). Not worth 15 lost recall points. `leaf_candidate_sum` saturates ~409k in n128
  vs ~398k n1024 at np96 — similar candidate volume reached at far higher scoring cost.
