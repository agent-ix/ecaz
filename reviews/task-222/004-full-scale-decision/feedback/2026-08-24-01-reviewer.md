### 4. Three cleanups you have not seen

These come from `2026-08-24-01` feedback on
`plan/distann-materialization-followups`, which landed **after** you forked
`task222-recovery` from `0276d4a07`. They are not ignored findings.

- **Use `copyObject`, not `palloc` + `copy_nonoverlapping`.** The shallow
  memcpy in `begin_custom_scan` produces a `CustomScan` that aliases every
  sublist with the original and is not a legal node copy. It works today
  because only EXPLAIN and our own code read it; `copyObject` costs nothing at
  scan init and removes the class of question.
- **Comment why the split tree is safe.** After `(*node).ss.ps.plan` is
  swapped to the copy, the parent Limit's `plan->lefttree` still points at the
  original and shares the *original* target list. It is safe because the junk
  filter reads `resjunk` positionally and never evaluates that list — three
  non-obvious facts that need one comment.
- **Derive the mask once.** `payload_mask_for_custom_scan` runs on the
  original and again on the copy with identical inputs before any rewrite.
