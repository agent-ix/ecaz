## Concerns to address before this becomes input to AWS / Graviton v4

### 1. Title-vs-evidence: "Graviton v4" is the goal; this packet is WSL2 local

The packet is correctly scoped as local-only (`AWS: not used`, "local
results only promote an experiment to AWS"), and promotion language is
honest. But the surrounding task is specifically about Graviton v4
latency, and the bits=1 NEON byte-LUT kernel work (the literal reason
Task 51 exists) is invisible on a non-Neoverse host. Two implications:

- Do not pick a promotion winner between `nlists=64` and `nlists=128`
  on local WSL2 numbers alone. The local data point can rule out
  *clearly bad* geometries, not pick the best one for m8g.
- The next packet manifest should explicitly call out that local results
  do not measure NEON byte-LUT behavior, and that the matched cell on
  m8g.2xlarge may invert the local p50 ordering. Otherwise reviewers
  downstream will read the local "n=64 at nprobe=8 = 7.91 ms" line as
  Graviton evidence.

### 2. Recall ceiling is saturated; "matched recall" claim is weak

At 10k DBpedia, every cell with `nprobe ≥ 16` (or `nprobe ≥ 32` for
n=128) returns recall@10 = 1.0000 in `suite-report.log`. The headline
table picks the lowest nprobe per geometry, where recall has just barely
fallen off the ceiling (0.9985 / 0.9970 / 0.9970). That means:

- The three headline rows are *not* matched-recall — they are picked at
  the first cell below 1.0, which the task plan calls out as a method
  problem ("No final claim of 'beats vchord' unless recall bands are
  actually matched"). The 25%-p50 promotion gate in Experiment 2 of
  `plan/tasks/51` is a *matched-recall* gate. This screen can't satisfy
  it.
- nprobe=8 across `nlists=32/64/128` is fixed-nprobe, not fixed-work:
  it probes 25% / 12.5% / 6.25% of lists respectively. The latency
  delta partly reflects that. State this explicitly in `request.md` so
  the headline isn't read as "n=64 is 1.7× faster at the same recall".
- The geometry signal cannot be separated from the ceiling on a 10k
  fixture. The next-larger local suite must be at least 100k (and
  preferably 1M, since 1M is the AWS frontier in Task 51 cost
  decomposition) for nprobe choices to mean anything.

### 3. q-count = 200 falls under task-plan floor of 500 with no recorded waiver

`plan/tasks/51` §1 acceptance criteria say "q-count at least 500 unless
cost forces a documented smaller run." Suite `defaults.queries_limit`
is 200 and neither `manifest.md` nor `request.md` cites a cost reason.
For a 10k screen this is defensible, but record the waiver in
`manifest.md` so the next packet can re-evaluate the floor for 100k/1M
and so this is not silently inherited.

### 4. Suite `bits: 4` vs index `quant_bits=1` needs a one-line manifest note

`suite.json` carries `bits: 4` in defaults and in each step, while the
load reloptions carry `quant_bits=1`. There is a
`suite-audit-after-bits-fix.log` referenced from `manifest.md` whose
content is just `audit passed: 12 steps`, so the rationale for the two
different "bits" knobs is not preserved anywhere a future reviewer can
find. Add a one-line `manifest.md` note: e.g., "suite-level `bits` is
the recall/latency LUT scoring width; index storage is RaBitQ
`quant_bits=1`." If `bits=4` is the byte-LUT chunked path and that is
what is being measured, say so — the byte-LUT work is one of the things
Task 51 is supposed to validate.

### 5. Scan counters (Baseline #4) not yet captured

Task 51 §"Baseline and Required Cleanup" #4 requires postings
visited/scored, posting pages read, candidates emitted, duplicates
filtered, heap rerank rows/blocks, and approximate-vs-rerank time
"before changing behavior." This screen captures none of those. That is
fine for this packet's stated scope, but the geometry recommendation
("favor n=64 and n=128") is in fact a behavior change input. The packet
should either (a) defer the promotion decision until the counter pass
runs against the larger local suite, or (b) state explicitly in
`manifest.md` that the counter pass is still owed and that no AWS gate
will run until it lands.

### 6. nprobe sweep skips the recall-loss region for `nlists=32` and `nlists=64`

`nlists=32` sweeps `[8,16,24,32]` (25%–100% of lists); `nlists=64`
sweeps `[8,16,24,32,48,64]` (12.5%–100%). Neither sweep visits a
nprobe below the recall-saturation point, so we cannot see where
recall begins to drop. Only `nlists=128` (sweep starts at `nprobe=8`,
6.25%) actually crosses the knee (`0.9935` at nprobe=8). Without
sub-knee data points on n=32 and n=64, the suite cannot tell you the
shape of the recall curve. The next packet should sweep below the
knee on every geometry it carries forward, even if those cells are
"obviously bad" — they are what defines matched-recall comparison.

## Recommendation

- Land the small `manifest.md` clarifications above (bits semantics,
  q-count waiver, explicit Graviton non-coverage note, deferred
  counters note, "headline rows are not matched-recall") as a small
  amend or follow-up packet — they don't require a re-run.
- Do not treat n=64 vs n=128 as decided. Carry both forward into the
  100k (and then 1M) local suite, add nprobe values below the recall
  knee, and capture postings/heap-blocks/exact-rerank counters in that
  next packet.
- Do not promote any local result to an AWS m8g run until (a) the
  matched-recall comparison can be made at 100k or 1M, and (b) the
  Baseline #4 counter pass exists. Per the amended strategy section
  of `plan/tasks/51`, the latency gap to vchord is dominated by heap
  fetch + toast detoast, which the geometry sweep cannot move — so
  the geometry experiment's promotion bar (≥25% p50 at matched recall)
  should be evaluated against counters, not just wall-clock p50.

Packet is acceptable as a first local screen. It is not yet sufficient
to inform a Graviton v4 decision.
