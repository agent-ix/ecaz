## Recall-gap assessment

vchord sits at 0.9995 recall@10; ecaz nprobe=256 is at 0.9936. The
0.006-point gap is closable, in order of expected leverage:

1. **rabitq8c4 sidecar replacing rabitq1 in-AM rerank** — the single
   biggest known lever. 8-bit codes give much tighter score
   discrimination than the current 1-bit in-AM rerank. The sidecar
   packet shows `rabitq8c4` at 0.98 recall with `candidate_k=50`;
   widening candidate_k and pairing it with the IVF candidate scan
   should push recall to ~0.995–0.998 at modest latency cost.
2. **Adaptive nprobe** (`task51-ivf-adaptive-nprobe`, already in
   flight) — exactly the lever for closing tail-recall gaps without
   raising average probe cost.
3. Higher fixed nprobe (cheap, saturating).
4. k-means++ init + more iterations (small recall lift, no query
   cost).
5. Larger `nlists` than √N (more build cost, no query cost).

Combining (1) and (2) is the realistic path to ≥0.999 at competitive
latency. (1) is the highest-leverage because the sidecar harness and
the rabitq8c4 variant both already exist.
