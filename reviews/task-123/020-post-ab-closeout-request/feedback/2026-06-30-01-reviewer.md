**1. Does 019 satisfy the packet-018 required b2/b4 engaged prune A/B? —

My 018 sign-off required two things from the A/B: (a) recall must hold at the
prune-off value, and (b) the candidate-count / materialization effect must now

- (a) **Met.** recall@10 = 1.0000 with prune ON across every variant at

- (b) **Not demonstrated.** I diffed prune-on vs prune-off in full. The *only*

  `payload_rows_sum=2000`, `payload_bytes_sum=24632000`,
  `remote_heap_candidate_sum=6000`, `compact_candidate_sum=6000`,
  `merge_input_sum=6000`, `merge_output_sum=2000`. The pre-materialization prune

  engaged-but-ineffective prune from a still-inert one. In every observable

This does not block the closeout (see below), but it means we have **not**
measured prune efficacy — only its harmlessness.

The distinction matters for the carried-forward work: do not let the record
calcify into "we proved pre-materialization prune doesn't help." We proved it
doesn't *hurt* and doesn't move coordinator latency; its drop rate is still
unmeasured.
