## The correctness trap for Phase 4 — read before wiring the LazyRerankBound

Your "Next" plan says you'll derive the calibrated `LazyRerankBound` for RaBitQ
"from surface (1)." **Be careful: surface (1) is the wrong reference for 112's
lazy rerank stop, and reusing it blindly would be a recall bug.**

- **Posting prune (Phases 2/3)** operates entirely in *quantized* space: it
  prunes against a frontier built from quantized estimates, so a bound on the
  *estimate* is the correct and sound thing. ✓
- **112's lazy rerank stop** operates against *exact* scores: `worst_kept` is an
  exact heap-f32 neg-IP, and to skip an un-reranked candidate you need a sound
  lower bound on its **exact** neg-IP, not on its estimate. Surface (1) bounds
  the estimate `α·⟨q,x_dec⟩`; the exact score is `−⟨q,o⟩`, and
  `⟨q,o⟩ = ⟨q,x_dec⟩ + ⟨q, o−x_dec⟩`. The residual term is exactly the
  quantization error surface (1) does **not** bound.

So the Phase-4 bound must be a deterministic bound on the **exact** score. The
good news: one may be constructible without the probabilistic envelope —
`|⟨q, o−x_dec⟩| ≤ ||q||·||o − x_dec||`, and `||o − x_dec||` is deterministically
recoverable from the stored norms/o_dot (`||o−x_dec||² = ||o||² −
2·o_dot·||o||·||x_dec|| + ||x_dec||²`). That yields a *sound* exact lower bound
distinct from both surface (1) and the ε-envelope. The open question is
**tightness**: for 1-bit RaBitQ `||o − x_dec||` is large, so `||q||·||o−x_dec||`
may be too loose for the stop to fire often — in which case 112's lazy exact stop
is real but rarely-firing, or not worth it. That is a legitimate empirical
finding for the deferred bench.

**Required of Phase 4:** (a) derive the exact-score bound correctly (do not
substitute surface (1)); (b) keep it deterministic/sound — if only a
probabilistic exact bound exists, the recall-safe 112 stop is **not** achievable
and you must document that as the negative finding rather than force a
probabilistic stop (Non-Goal); (c) carry the two seam fixes from
`reviews/task-112/001-.../feedback/2026-06-19-01-reviewer.md` (trait
monotonicity precondition; `worst_kept.is_finite()` guard + true exact-score
feeding + k-cap/on-demand-suffix). The 113 posting-prune win stands on its own
regardless of how the 112 rerank integration lands.
