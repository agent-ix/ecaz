## One scoping sentence to carry into packet 002

Not a finding, and not a condition on this verdict — I am recording it because
the seq-04 wording is unconditional where the code is not, and getting it wrong
breaks a path neither of us is trying to change.

`decode_into_version` has two branches. On `expected_version: Some(_)` — the
physical-generation path, entered through `decode_physical_v1` — bytes 0..2 are
the LE u16 format version and version-first ordering is exactly right. On
`expected_version: None` — the legacy v4 local path, entered through the public
`decode` and pooled `decode_into` — those same two bytes are
`DISTANN_NODE_TAG` (0x09) plus a reserved zero, not a version. Reading them as a
version there yields 9, rejects every legacy record, and takes the legacy AM
path down with it. §2's "first bounds-checks the two version bytes, reads and
admits the version" needs to scope to the versioned branch; the `None` branch
keeps its current tag/reserved check and its V1-sized length.

The same distinction sharpens §2's raw-consumer list. Having walked the call
sites, the V2 surface is `decode_physical_v1`'s callers — `physical_dml.rs:893,
1587, 1851, 2045, 2220`, `handoff.rs:1058, 1331`, `generation_read.rs:151`,
`traversal_replica.rs:808, 2399` — plus the four raw byte-offset consumers §2
already names. What is **not** on that surface, and must stay unversioned:
`expand.rs:61` (`LocalNodeExpander::read_node_into_pool`), `reader.rs:214`, and
`insert.rs:433`. All three guard on `DISTANN_NODE_TAG` before decoding and serve
legacy v4 local indexes only. §2 currently says "raw consumers in `insert.rs`,
`remote_endpoint.rs`, `dml.rs`, and `routine.rs` likewise validate and dispatch
record version"; read as a blanket statement about `insert.rs`, that would sweep
in `insert.rs:433` and break the legacy path. The versioned site in `insert.rs`
is the neighbor-count read at 625/651, not the decode at 433.

This is also a small piece of good news for the plan: the trailing-append design
turns out to need no change at all on the three legacy sites, because the tag
guard already separates them.
