## For packet 004: the counters prove laziness, not the mechanism

The counters establish that exact-vector reads touch only the hot tier. They do
**not** establish the thing the whole task rests on: that the hot read is one
inline page fetch instead of a heap fetch plus a TOAST index descent plus chunk
fetches. `ExactVectorReads` and `ExactVectorBytes` look identical whether the
vector was inline or detoasted.

Structurally the property holds — seq-06 pins `attstorage='p'` at DDL and proves
the formed tuple is 6,204 bytes inline, and you cannot detoast what was never
toasted. But packet 004 has to *attribute* the result, not just report it. Decide
now how: either a detoast/TOAST-fetch counter, or per-relation block statistics
that include each tier's TOAST relation, so the control arm's TOAST reads and the
candidate's absence of them appear side by side. Without that, a win is
observed but unexplained, and a loss is uninterpretable.

This folds naturally into the shared-buffer hit ratio metric already carried.

## Conditions for closing packet 002

Packet-001 §7 checkpoint 4 requires PG18 coverage of: id-only, hot scalar, exact
vector, cold scalar, mixed, `SELECT *`, **NULL**, external TOAST, qual-only,
deepening, rescan, **restart**, and **owner failure**.

This slice claims id-only, hot, cold, mixed, whole-row, qual-only,
cached-parameter, correlated-rescan, deepening, forced intent retry,
local/remote ownership, and externally TOASTed cold values. That leaves three
unaccounted:

1. **NULL** — a projection returning a NULL cold or hot scalar value.
2. **Restart** — generation readable across a backend restart.
3. **Owner failure** — read behavior when the owning participant is unavailable.

Restart and owner failure may reasonably belong with packet 003's lifecycle
matrix; NULL does not, and it is cheap. Either add them here or state explicitly
in the packet-003 request that checkpoint 4's restart/owner-failure items moved
there, so the requirement is not silently dropped between packets. Packet 002
should not be marked closed until that reconciliation is written down.
