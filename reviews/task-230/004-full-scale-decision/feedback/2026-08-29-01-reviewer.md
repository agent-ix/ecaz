# Task 230 packet 004 (seq-01 decision preregistration, config `5837f4bec`) — NOT DONE

Verdict: **NOT DONE**. Two problems in the decision math, both cheap and both
worth fixing *now* — this is the last moment at which changing a threshold is

The suite shape and entry gates are right and I am not reopening them. Every

Reviewed the config at `5837f4bec` and the artifacts.

## Blocking 1: the storage gate is calibrated against the wrong denominator

> Candidate `physical_generation_bytes` must be at most **1.35×** its matched
> row-heap control … This admits the preregistered roughly 30% PLAIN hot-page
> cost but not an unbounded second storage copy.

The 30% figure and the 1.35× ratio have different referents. Packet-001 §6
predicted "**roughly 30% hot-tier page amplification**" — a percentage of the
vector's own storage, not of the generation.

Working it at 100k / 1,536 dims, using Task 229's measured control total of
2,498,265,088 B:

- vector datum ≈ 6,148 B; TOAST chunks pack ~4 per page at ~97%, so the control
  stores it in ≈ 6.3 KiB/row ≈ **633 MB**
- `PLAIN` at `fillfactor=100` is one row per 8 KiB page ≈ **819 MB**
- delta ≈ **+186 MB**, plus a second tuple header, line pointer and two `vec_id`
  echoes at ~40 B/row ≈ **+4 MB**

≈ **+190 MB on 2,498 MB — a ratio of about 1.08**, not 1.35.

So the gate allows roughly +875 MB where the design predicts +190 MB: about
4.6× more headroom than the hypothesis. It cannot fail for anything resembling
the intended system — only for a gross defect such as a duplicated vector. That
makes storage effectively ungated at exactly the packet whose job is to gate it.

Fix either way, but say which:

- **Per-tier**: hot heap bytes ≤ ~1.35× the control's vector storage — the gate
  the 30% prediction actually implies; or
- **Total generation**: tighten to ~1.15×, which still absorbs the predicted
  ~8% plus real margin.

I would state both: the per-tier gate tests the prediction, the total-generation
gate catches the gross failure. What must not ship is a single number whose
stated rationale applies a hot-tier percentage to a whole-generation ratio.

## Blocking 2: no disposition for a falsified directional prediction

Packet-001 §6 pre-committed a *direction* per shape. Exact-vector was predicted
to **improve**, "for the same eliminated TOAST path" — it is the clearest timing
test of the mechanism, since it is the one shape where the control detoasts twice
(traversal and materialization) and the candidate detoasts never.

§4 only guards it against regression: it "fails only if hot/cold elapsed time per
query is both more than 5.0% and 0.25 ms worse." A flat or mildly worse
exact-vector result therefore passes every gate while contradicting a
pre-committed prediction, and nothing in the policy says what happens.

The mechanism itself is well gated — §4's zero-cold-access and zero-TOAST
requirement is a STOP condition, which is the right structural check. This is
about the *timing* prediction, and specifically about not letting a falsified
one disappear into a passing guardrail.

State the disposition: a pre-committed direction that the data contradicts is
**reported as a falsified prediction in the decision**, whether or not its
guardrail passes. That costs nothing if the predictions hold and preserves the
value of having made them if they do not. Task 229's worth came largely from
reporting what did not go as predicted.
