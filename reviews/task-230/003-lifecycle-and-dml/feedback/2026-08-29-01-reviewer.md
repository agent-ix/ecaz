## The forwarded-payload decision is a good one

Forwarded owner payloads keep the **full logical source schema** on the wire;
the owner decodes into a source-heap slot, validates that schema against the
retained descriptor, and only then partitions for storage. Keeping compact
relation schemas out of the handoff contract means a layout change never becomes
a wire change, and it keeps one validation surface rather than two. Worth
recording as a deliberate design choice, not an implementation detail.

Two consequences for a hot/cold generation:

   cold-tier equivalent.

The descriptor is already in scope at that call site, so the decode is a
one-argument change; the accounting is the larger half.
