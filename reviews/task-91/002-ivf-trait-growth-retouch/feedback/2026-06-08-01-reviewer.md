## Finding — confirm PqFastScan parity coverage shape

The packet lists `PqFastScan` as the fourth required bit-exact parity shape.
The diff shows the existing PqFastScan tests were refactored to **route
through** `IvfQuantCodec` (using `dispatch.quant_codec_with_pq_model(&model)`
and `QuantCodec::encode_source` / `score_ip_batch`), but I cannot tell from
the diff alone whether the refactored test still asserts
`f32::to_bits()` equality against the **pre-retouch** scorer path
(`encode_source_with_pq_model` + `prepare_ip_query_with_pq_model` called
directly on `dispatch`), or whether it only re-asserts internal consistency
of the new path.

The other three shapes have explicit
`assert_eq!(batch_scores[i].to_bits(), scalar.to_bits(), ...)` loops
against the direct scalar scorer. Please either:

1. Add a dedicated
   `common_quant_codec_pq_fastscan_batch_is_bit_exact_with_scalar` test that
   scores one batch through `IvfQuantCodec` and a parallel batch through
   the pre-retouch direct path, asserting `to_bits()` equality; **or**
2. In the next packet, point to the specific assertion in the existing
   `pq_fastscan_*` tests that locks bit-exactness against the pre-retouch
   path, so reviewers can verify F2 coverage is complete for all four
   shapes.

This is a small documentation/coverage gap, not a behavior defect. The
SPIRE/HNSW/DiskANN phases ride on the IVF Phase 2 reference; locking the
PqFastScan parity claim now avoids ambiguity later.
