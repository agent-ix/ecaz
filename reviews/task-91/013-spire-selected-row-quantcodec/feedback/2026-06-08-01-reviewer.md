    ✓

  column_format, gamma, encoded_payload, min_ip_to_keep)`:
  - When `min_ip_to_keep` is `Some(…)` it forwards to
    `scorer.try_score_payload_ip(...)`, preserving the recall-safe
    upper-bound prune.

    wraps in `Some`.
  - That `Option<f32>` contract matches what the caller's branch needs
    so the prune-or-score logic in
    `append_quantized_v2_filtered_column_candidates` can stay one
    shape. ✓
