## Finding — confirm equivalence at the next SPIRE follow-up

The trait `score_ip_batch` builds its `codec` from a snapshot of
`payload_format()` + `dimensions()` at scan time, rather than holding a
reference to the scorer's own internal codec state. That is fine for the
current scorer where those values are stable across the scorer's lifetime,
but the SPIRE Phase 3 closeout packet (or the next SPIRE slice) should
explicitly confirm:

- the `SpireAssignmentQuantCodec` produced by `scorer.quant_codec()` returns
  identical `codec_kind` / `search_codec_tag` / `payload_len` to the scorer's
  own implicit format-state, on every supported format; and
- there is no live mutation of `payload_format` or `dimensions` between
  `quant_codec()` construction and the `score_ip_batch` call.

These should both be true today; calling them out in the closeout protects
against a future refactor accidentally introducing drift.
