## Minor (non-blocking) — `GammaAndResidualSigns` accepted but signs ignored

The shared helper accepts both `CandidateMeta::Gamma` and
`CandidateMeta::GammaAndResidualSigns { gamma, .. }`, taking the gamma
and reading residual signs from the packed payload bytes in both
cases. Per the packet-001 seq 03 decision, no current storage surface
produces the split variant — so accepting it is forward-compat, but
silently ignoring its `signs` field is a foot-gun: a future caller
passing meta-signs would get payload-signs scoring with no error.
Either reject `GammaAndResidualSigns` until a surface actually
produces it, or add a debug assertion / comment at the acceptance site
stating the signs field is intentionally unused and payload bytes are
authoritative. One-line fix; fold into any next code packet.
