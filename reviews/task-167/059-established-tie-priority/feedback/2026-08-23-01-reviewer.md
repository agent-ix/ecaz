## 1. The heldout gate is misapplied (P1)

Four 50k heldout results against `allowed_deficit=0.007000`:

| Packet | Candidate | physical | fresh | deficit | AC-4 population (band 0.015) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 047 | shipped robust-prune | `0.848722` | `0.857333` | `0.008611` | pass |
| 051 | append-when-room | `0.846722` | `0.857333` | `0.010611` | pass (`0.013600`) |
| 054 | conservative admission | `0.847722` | `0.857333` | `0.009611` | pass (`0.007316`) |
| 057 | full-target no-op | `0.847722` | `0.857333` | `0.009611` | pass (`0.008970`) |

Two things follow that the packets do not draw out:

- **AC-4 is satisfied at 50k by the shipped default.** Every row passes the
  inserted-neighborhood band. The task has been held open by a gate on the
  population the AC explicitly excludes.
- **The candidates are not distinguishable.** 054 and 057 are bit-identical at
  `0.847722`; 057's request already establishes its change was inert.
  `0.846722` to `0.848722` is a 4-slot spread across four different graph
  policies. No mechanism is being measured at this resolution — only whether a
  fixed constant happens to fall above or below a near-flat line.

The gate is not worthless: as a **regression detector** it is exactly what
packet 043 §2 asked for, and it is correctly implemented (hard band, no
widening, structured emission, unit-pinned). The defect is its semantics.
Fix the semantics, keep the machinery.

**Required change:** make the band per-scale and baseline-relative. For each
scale, record the shipped-default deficit as that scale's baseline and set
`band = baseline_deficit + 2 * sd(physical arm at that scale)`. Fail the step
when a change moves the deficit **beyond its own scale's baseline band** —
which is what catches "a future change halves post-insert recall," the thing
the gate exists for. It does not, and should not, encode a claim that the
absolute physical-vs-fresh deficit must be ≤ 0.007 at all scales; nothing in
FR-083 or NFR-020 makes that claim, and the packets' own data contradicts it.

## 2. Packet 059's code — correct, land it (P1, unblocks)

`350385ce9` is right on its own merits and should not be gated on the 50k
heldout number at all.

The physical backlink path built its robust-prune union proposal-first
(`physical_dml.rs:1748-1752`, pre-change) while the pure/local planner and
batch Vamana place established neighbors first. `robust_prune` breaks
exact-distance ties by candidate ordinal, so the two paths could select
different edge sets from identical input. Physical and local planners
disagreeing on the same input is a divergence bug independent of any recall
measurement, and AC-4 compares specifically against a batch rebuild — matching
batch's ordering is the correct target, not an arbitrary tie convention.

The fix is the right shape: one `select_insert_backlink_neighbors` helper
(`insert.rs:129-142`) that owns the union order, both planners routed through
it, and `backlink_exact_tie_prefers_the_established_neighbor`
(`insert.rs:958-980`) pinning the property in both directions — it asserts
proposal-first *does* pick the duplicate, so the test fails if the helper is
bypassed. `ddea621a6` is labels only.

Expect this to move the 50k heldout number by roughly nothing. That is fine.
Land it as a correctness alignment; do not attach a quality verdict to it.
