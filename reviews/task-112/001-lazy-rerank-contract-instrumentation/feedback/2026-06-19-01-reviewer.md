## Two concrete findings the 113 implementor MUST carry (seam answers)

You asked whether `LazyRerankBound`/`k`-cap is the right shape for 113. Mostly
yes, with two latent traps to fix when the bound goes live — flag them now so
they don't become a silent recall bug:

1. **State monotonicity as a trait precondition.** The "check the suffix head
   only" optimization (`suffix_head_lb >= worst_kept`) is sound *only* because
   `lower_bound` is non-decreasing in approx score, so the head carries the
   smallest bound in the suffix. `NoBound` (constant) and `SlackBound` (affine)
   satisfy this; a calibrated 113 bound MUST too, or the head-only check is
   unsound and you'd have to scan all of `S`. Today this is a doc note in the fn
   body; it should be a documented **precondition on the `LazyRerankBound`
   trait** that implementors uphold.

2. **The placeholder fetch + the `−inf >= −inf` edge.** `scan.rs` feeds
   `|_| NEG_INFINITY` into the driver. That is inert *today only because*
   `floor == width` so the predicate is never reached — but note `f32::NEG_INFINITY
   >= f32::NEG_INFINITY` is `true`. The moment 113 lowers `min_kept` (the k-cap)
   it MUST also stop feeding the placeholder and feed **true exact scores** into
   the driver (the packet's "fetch the mandatory floor, evaluate, then fetch the
   remainder" shape). Otherwise `worst_kept` stays −∞ and the stop fires
   spuriously, skipping live candidates → recall collapse. Recommend hardening
   the driver itself: gate the early stop on `worst_kept.is_finite()` (only stop
   once a real kept floor exists). That makes `NoBound` robust even if a future
   caller mis-sets the floor, and removes the placeholder landmine. (Can't edit
   src/ as reviewer — flagging for the coder.)
