## What the per-trial data reveals

control-10k, five trials per arm:

| arm | mean rows/s | sd | CV | min–max | spread |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| physical (3-node) | 0.8681 | 0.0772 | **8.89%** | 0.773–0.978 | 23.6% |
| single-node control | 7.0868 | 0.0983 | 1.39% | 6.935–7.176 | 3.4% |

The distributed arm is **six times noisier** than the single-node arm — which is
what you would expect from a three-owner 2PC round with per-transaction fsync
and lock waits, and it is the arm the A/B actually compares.

At CV ≈ 8.9% with n = 5, the 95% confidence half-width on the physical mean is
**±11.0%**. Comparing two such means, the smallest difference this design can
distinguish from noise is roughly **15%**.

Task 234 measured the read-side equivalent of this wrapper at +7.1% to +17.5%.
**A write-side cost in that same range would be invisible at n = 5.** The run in
flight will produce six numbers whose null result means "we could not see it",
not "it is not there" — and those read identically on a task header a month
from now.

(Caveat stated plainly: an sd from five samples is itself imprecise. But the
point does not rest on the exact figure — the observed 23.6% min-to-max spread
in a single arm is on its own larger than the effect being hunted.)

## Sizing, and why 10k only

For two independent means at CV ≈ 8.9%, 80% power, α = 0.05:

| detect | trials per arm | ~wall clock per arm at 10k |
|---|---:|---:|
| 15% | 6 | ~4 min |
| 10% | 13 | ~8 min |
| 5% | 50 | ~31 min |

`TASK167_AB_ROWS_PER_TRIAL = 32` at 0.868 rows/s is ~37 s per physical trial at
10k. Fifty trials per arm at 10k is about an hour for both arms — cheap, and it
buys 5% resolution.

Do this at **10k only**. The wrapper's cost is per-statement (BEGIN, mutation,
PREPARE, intent write, COMMIT PREPARED), so it is present at every scale, while
per-trial wall clock grows ~2.6× from 10k to 100k (0.336 vs 0.868 rows/s). 10k
is the cheapest place to buy resolution, and 50k/100k from the current re-run
remain useful as scale-trend evidence at their existing precision.
