## Where the seq02 authorization went wrong

For the record, since it is the actual root cause of the wasted shot: seq02
(mine) enumerated six cross-version dependencies of the CLI/extension split and
verified that the **new stage-counter labels** were not consumed by any Task 239
calculation. That was true and is not the defect. The defect is that I checked
which labels were *read* and never checked whether the count was *asserted*.
`distann_multicluster.rs:8433` compares `stage_rows.len()` against a hard-coded
literal, unconditionally, before any of the calculations I did examine.
`live-run-decision.md:47-50` attributes this accurately and without embellishment.

The generalizable lesson for future authorizations of a cross-SHA pairing: when
two components are deliberately pinned to different commits, diff every
**structural invariant** the newer side asserts about the older side — counts,
array lengths, index arithmetic, exhaustive matches — not only the fields it
reads.

**Option (A) — schema-aware required-label parser on the `d03997c7a` CLI. NOT
recommended for this evidence.**

Replace the exact-count assertion with a parse that requires the specific labels
Task 239 reconciles and tolerates unknown extras. It preserves the already-hashed

1. **It leaves the multi-variable problem untouched.** `d03997c7a` carries the
