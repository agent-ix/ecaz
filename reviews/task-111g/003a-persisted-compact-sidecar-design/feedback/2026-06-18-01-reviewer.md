## Verdict

Good call stopping at a design checkpoint — both forks are durable on-disk
decisions where guessing wrong wastes a large implementation, and the task
authorized the 003a/003b split for exactly this. The analysis is sound. **All
three decisions are blessed**, with one scope correction and a Task-42 hard
requirement. Proceed to 003b.

## Decisions

1. **Option B over Option A: blessed.** Option A (thread `rerank_tid` through the
   coalesced SoA candidate path) is correctly rejected — it would restructure
   coalescing, which the task forbids. Option B (compact sidecar keyed by heap
   TID, new tag `0x2A`, read tid-sorted at rerank) touches only the rerank read
   path and leaves the coarse stage / `0x25`/`0x28` / coalescing / quant math
   untouched. Right boundary.

   **Scope correction (my error to fix, not yours):** persisting a compact rep in
   the AM *is* index-side storage — there is no real "table-side persistence" for
   an index AM (the packet-005 "table-side sidecar" was an *external bench table*,
   not an AM feature). So Option B **is** `rerank_placement='index'`, and the
   contract already has that knob (currently rejected). 003b should therefore
   **implement `rerank_placement='index'` as the persisted `0x2A` sidecar and
   lift its rejection**; `'table'` stays the heap-source path (the 001
   on-the-fly/heap_f32 read). My task's "index-side deferred" line was based on a
   misconception — disregard it; index-side is the win.

2. **Byte-reduction-by-counter pg_test satisfies the code-slice win gate:
   blessed.** Add `stats_rerank_source_bytes_read` and a pg_test asserting
   index-placement f16/rabitq4 read fewer rerank source bytes than table f32.
   The full `ecaz bench suite` (latency) stays the packet-002 Phase-3 gate on a
   provisioned lane (env-blocked here) — that's the right split.

3. **Read mechanism: filtered tid-sorted survivor pass: blessed.** Survivors are
   bounded by `rerank_width`, and a single tid-sorted filtered pass matches the
   existing heap_f32 read shape. Prefer it over per-candidate lookup.
