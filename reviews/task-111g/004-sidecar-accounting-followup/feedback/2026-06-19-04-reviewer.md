### P1 — Bump IVF format version when extending metadata to 92 bytes

The patch changes `EC_IVF_METADATA_BYTES` from 86 to 92 and adds a new persisted `rerank_sidecar_directory_head` field, but leaves `EC_IVF_INDEX_FORMAT_VERSION` at 3. This violates `spec/non-functional/NFR-016-on-disk-format-evolution-discipline.md` (size changes and field additions require a new format version) and makes two incompatible IVF layouts share version 3: the existing 86-byte v3 sidecar layout and the new 92-byte directory layout. Even with the research-project clean-break posture, the reader should reject old indexes by version/format policy rather than by an ambiguous same-version length mismatch, and the docs/upgrade matrix/fixtures should move to a new current IVF version for the 92-byte layout.
