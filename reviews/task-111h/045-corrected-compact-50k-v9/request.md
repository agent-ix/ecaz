Immediate 50k/w64 readouts:

- Source f32 and index f16 had identical recall across the sweep: recall@10
  0.9820 at nprobe 64 and 0.9985 at nprobe 200.
- Index f16 was not faster than source f32 at nprobe 64 in this slice
  (5.57 ms versus 5.54 ms), and it used much more ec_ivf index storage
  (166.70 MiB versus 13.80 MiB).
- RaBitQ-4 clips 2/3/4 did not hit recall@10 >= 0.97 or >= 0.99; best was
  `rq4 est c3` / `rq4 exact c3` at recall@10 0.9605 at nprobe 200.
- RaBitQ-8 clip 4 was the only compact quantized cell to hit recall@10 >=
  0.99: 0.9915 at nprobe 128 and 0.9930 at nprobe 200.
- RaBitQ-8 clip 4 remained materially smaller than index f16
  (93.40 MiB versus 166.70 MiB ec_ivf index) and was faster at the first
  0.99-recall nprobe in this slice (5.99 ms at nprobe 128 versus index f16
  7.59 ms).
- TurboQuant exact-dequant did not improve recall over default in this slice
  and was slower at nprobe 64.
