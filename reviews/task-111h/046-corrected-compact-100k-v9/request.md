Immediate 100k/w64 readouts:

- Source f32 and index f16 are the only cells that hit recall@10 >= 0.97 at
  nprobe 64 and recall@10 >= 0.99 at nprobe 128.
- Index f16 was not faster than source f32 at nprobe 64 in this slice
  (8.16 ms versus 7.72 ms), and it used much more ec_ivf index storage
  (330.10 MiB versus 24.60 MiB).
- RaBitQ-4 clips 2/3/4 did not hit recall@10 >= 0.97 or >= 0.99; best was
  `rq4 est c3` at recall@10 0.9530 at nprobe 200.
- RaBitQ-8 clip 4 was the only compact quantized cell to hit recall@10 >=
  0.99: estimator reached 0.9915 and exact-dequant reached 0.9920 at nprobe
  200.
- At the 0.97 recall target, RaBitQ-8 clip 4 first qualifies at nprobe 128
  with 10.20 to 10.30 ms mean latency. Source f32 qualifies at nprobe 64 with
  7.72 ms; index f16 qualifies at nprobe 64 with 8.16 ms.
- At the 0.99 recall target, RaBitQ-8 clip 4 first qualifies at nprobe 200
  with 13.70 to 14.40 ms mean latency. Source f32 qualifies at nprobe 128 with
  11.40 ms; index f16 qualifies at nprobe 128 with 11.80 ms.
- TurboQuant exact-dequant did not improve recall over default in this slice
  and was slower at nprobe 64 and nprobe 200.
