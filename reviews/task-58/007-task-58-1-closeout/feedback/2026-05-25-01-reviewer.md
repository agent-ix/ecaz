## Why "machine-state noise" doesn't clear the gate

Coder's rationale:
> Why this is likely machine-state, not regression
> The Task 58.1 changes ... are [structural-only, no scan-path code]

Plausible but **not load-bearing**:

1. **Two consecutive runs ≠ random noise.** Random noise is
   uncorrelated; this is systematic +20-45% elevation across all
   sweep points in two back-to-back runs. That's a process change,
   not measurement variance.
2. **Stddev tripled.** If it were just slower baseline state, the
   means would shift but the stddev should track. Tripled stddev
   suggests something is contending with the scan threads —
   could be background process, could be inlining-pattern shift
   from Audit 1's closure-form ops causing register-pressure
   change. Either way, needs investigation.
3. **"No plausible regression mechanism" is an absence of proof,
   not proof of absence.** Closure-form ops can subtly affect
   monomorphization and inlining; the bench is supposed to detect
   exactly this kind of indirect effect.
4. **Per [[feedback_no_premature_task_close]] HARD RULE**:
   "documented burn-in targets" must be met. The bench gate is
   the documented target. A failed gate cannot be papered with
   "likely noise" without affirmative evidence (e.g., clean-machine
   re-run showing baseline-comparable numbers).
