### What the rationale actually says

The structural-ceiling breakdown is plausible on the surface:

| Category | Count | Reviewer assessment |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| PG-extern boundary (single FFI calls) | ~25 | Genuinely irreducible without new ParallelContext/Worker wrappers (which the rationale itself flags as a future task). |
| DSM accessor methods returning `&T`/`&mut T` | ~6 | **Avoidable** — `feedback_view_operations_not_accessors` mandates ops, not safe accessors. Migrating these is *metric-neutral* per the rationale, but the safe-accessor shape is itself an anti-pattern that should be fixed. The "metric-neutral" framing dodges the rule by saying it won't help the count — but the rule's about shape, not count. |
| `slice::from_raw_parts` over DSM arrays | ~10 | Already at wrapper boundary; consolidating moves the block but doesn't remove it. Genuinely structural. |
| LWLock function-pointer dispatch | ~6 | The dispatch is unsafe; wrapping moves the block. **But: can the dispatch table be typed?** A typed `DsmLockOps` view with safe `acquire_shared(&self, lock) -> SharedGuard` etc. would absorb 6 blocks into 1 wrapper, net -5. Worth one more pass. |
| Atomic/spinlock primitive | ~3 | At Task 52 P8 boundary. Structural. |
| Test fixtures (cfg test) | ~10 | Each tests a specific invariant. Consolidated. Structural. |
| Per-call `unsafe fn` delegation wraps | ~12 | **Some are avoidable** — unsafe-fn-inside-unsafe-fn doesn't need the inner block. Audit. |
| Tuple-decode at P6 boundary | ~12 | Already at Task 53 wrapper. Structural. |

Two categories above admit further work without structural refactor:

- **LWLock dispatch typing (-5 net)**: small refactor, real removal.

Pessimistic combined reduction: **84 → ~74-76**. Optimistic:
**~70-72**. That's close to or hitting the ≤78 floor with margin,
and possibly within reach of the ≤70 target.

**The "structural ceiling" disposition is premature.** At minimum,
