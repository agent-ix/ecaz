Key Result
----------

- Plain route overfetch is recall-positive: 50k reaches 1.0000 at nprobe 96,
  and 100k rises from 0.9310 at nprobe 32 to 0.9975 at nprobe 96.
- Plain route overfetch is expensive: 100k nprobe 96 reads 13.04 GB of local
  object payload over 200 queries and has p50/p95 of 66.596/96.757 ms.
- The 25k route-row budget preserves the nprobe 96 recall at 50k and 100k
  while reducing routed leaves and object reads:
  - 50k: 19,200 to 13,799 routes, 5.91 GB to 4.25 GB, recall stays 1.0000.
  - 100k: 19,200 to 6,315 routes, 13.04 GB to 4.30 GB, recall stays 0.9975.
- Looser 50k/75k route-row caps add work without recall improvement at 100k.
- The final candidate budget still limits exact heap rerank to 5,000 rows
  total for 200 queries in every variant.

Decision
--------

Carry route overfetch plus a tight routed-row budget forward as a candidate
Phase 5/AWS hypothesis. Do not promote it as a product default from this local
packet: latency remains high at 100k, the matrix is local-only, and distributed
near-data rerank/shipping evidence is still missing.
