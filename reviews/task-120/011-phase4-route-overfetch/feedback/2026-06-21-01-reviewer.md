## Does overfetch demonstrably recover the Phase-1 route/leaf recall loss, and at what cost?

**Yes, demonstrably, at the route stage, at a real latency/I-O cost.**
At 100k, route overfetch lifts recall@10 from 0.9310 (nprobe 32) to 0.9975
(nprobe 96). The cost is p50 26.121→66.596 ms (~2.5×), p95 32.602→96.757 ms
(~3×), and object payload 4.34 GB→13.04 GB over 200 queries. A tight 25k
routed-row budget then claws back most of the I/O (13.04 GB→4.30 GB, routes
19,200→6,315) while holding recall at 0.9975 and p50 at ~63.6 ms. So the
mechanism works and the trade-off is fully quantified — but at this scale it is
a recall-for-latency trade, not a free win, and the packet says so.
