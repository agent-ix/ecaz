## Decision

- `max_candidate_rows=10000` and `25000` are recall-neutral under default
  `rerank_width=25`; heap rerank stays at 5,000 rows total for 200 queries.
- Raising exact rerank width to 100 or 500 is recall-neutral at every measured
  scale and nprobe, while increasing local latency.
- The remaining recall gap is not fixed by deeper exact rerank over the routed
  local candidate frontier. Treat these knobs as diagnostics, not a product
  default, and move Phase 4 to route/topology refinement.
