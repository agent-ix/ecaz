## ARCH-07 rejection: supported, with one framing correction

The rejection is supported. The directly attributed serialization-adjacent
work is 0.071 ms/scan against a 4.1–5.0 ms target; the ten serial rounds
survive any codec change; and the replica strictly dominates ARCH-07 for this
residual because it removes the boundary rather than re-encoding traffic
across it. The ADR's honest concession — that an always-on binary service
might cut backend scheduling overhead, with reopen conditioned on a separate
isolating measurement — is the right disposition for the unattributed share.

**Finding F1 (P3, framing).** On this lane the three owners are same-host
PG18 instances, so the ~0.41–0.50 ms/round transport wait is dominated by
per-round backend service/scheduling/IPC, not wire RTT — loopback RTT is two
orders of magnitude smaller. Phrases like "sequential network dependency" /
"ten sequential RTTs" (also in the roadmap ledger's ARCH-07 line) slightly
overstate the wire component. This does not weaken the selection — a replica
removes the per-round boundary whatever its composition, and a genuinely
multi-host deployment only widens the replica's advantage — but Task 198's
Phase 3 reconciliation should keep per-round wait decomposition (wait per
round, rounds, straggler spread) in its report so the ARCH-07 reopen premise
("independently measured transport-service overhead vs RTT") stays testable
from the control arm rather than requiring a bespoke study.

**Finding F2 (P3, completeness).** The gate's family 4 is "dedicated
transport (ARCH-07, ARCH-08)", but the comparison silently narrows it to
ARCH-07; ARCH-08 (shared-memory/Unix-domain same-host transport) gets no
disposition line even though, per F1, same-host service wait is exactly what
it targets — on this bench lane it is arguably the strongest transport
family member. Add one line (packet or roadmap ledger) recording why it is
not selected: it is topology-specific, does not remove rounds for genuinely
remote owners, and would optimize a bench-lane artifact rather than the
deployment architecture. The reasoning is implied by the ADR's "topology-
specific path" ledger entry; make it explicit so the family disposition is
complete.

and the Phase 3 usefulness gate on end-to-end mean and tails — not traversal
