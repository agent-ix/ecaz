# Task 87 Packet 001 — Self-Correction: Kernel Deferral Approved Too Broadly

This addendum self-corrects the Phase 1 design approval in
`2026-06-08-01-reviewer.md`, which said:

> "The deferral of the 32-vector u8 nibble LUT kernel is also the
> right risk call — agreed, do not freeze a flat-slab layout before
> HNSW and DiskANN expose their real per-step batch sizes."

That approval was too broad. The Phase 1 argument — wait to land
the kernel until per-AM batch sizes are measured — was effectively
circular: scoring-share could not be measured because no kernel
landed. The Phase 6 closeout (packet 015) bears this out: the
original ≥2× scoring-share gate is not met on any AM, and HNSW/IVF
have no scoring-share instrumentation at all.

The deferral was defensible **only on HNSW**, where per-frontier
batches are 8–32 candidates and a 32-block kernel mostly hits scalar
tails. It was not defensible on:

- **SPIRE** (column chunks ≤ 256 candidates) — the closeout's
  +28–38% pipeline gains come from incidental amortization in
  `score_turboquant_no_qjl_4bit_batch`, not from a real 32-block
  kernel. The kernel would push further.
- **IVF** (posting-list chunks 64–256 candidates) — the strongest
  case for the kernel landing in Task 87. The +1.8–9.7% wins on
  TurboQuant cells are plumbing only; the original 2× gate was
  achievable here with a real kernel.

Phase 1 should have said: **land the 32-block kernel on SPIRE + IVF
where batch widths justify it; defer on HNSW pending measurement
once the kernel exists.** Instead it deferred everywhere, and the
reviewer (me) approved that broad call without challenging the
circular reasoning.

This addendum does not invalidate the Phase 1 work that did land
(`CandidateBatch` data-flow abstraction, the per-AM batch-routing
plumbing on existing scorers, the measurement toggles). It
reopens the kernel decision under the original task wording:

> "Optionally one new 32-block u8 nibble LUT kernel
> (TurboVec-style); whether to land it in Task 87 vs a follow-up
> depends on the Phase 1 design call."

The Phase 1 design call is reversed. See feedback on packet 015
(`2026-06-08-02-reviewer.md`) for the reopen directive and Phase 7
plan.
