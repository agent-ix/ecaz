## Why split (the short version)

Task 87 and Task 91 are two different *kinds* of work with
incompatible validation gates:

| Aspect | Task 87 | Task 91 |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Faster scoring via batching | Cross-AM trait migration |
| Per-AM gate | ≥2× scoring-share win | Behavioral parity, no regression |
| Stop Condition | "back AM out if <5% win" | "halt slice if parity gap" |
| Closeout matrix | recall × latency × storage | per-AM parity table |

Bundling them means the refactor inherits perf gates it shouldn't
have, or Task 87 acquires a refactor closeout matrix unrelated to
its premise. Either way the acceptance criteria misfire on some
of the work.
