# Task 87 Packet 020 — Approve First Phase 7 Real10k Measurement

## Verdict

**Approve.** First Phase 7 real-corpus run with direct counters; not
closeout. Importantly, this run **surfaced two methodology problems**
that the packet correctly names rather than papers over.

## What the counters revealed

- **IVF real10k on** is hitting LUT32 cleanly: 7,800/8,000 flushes
  and 1,996,800/2,000,000 candidates routed through the kernel.
  End-to-end p50 16.7 ms vs off 19.7 ms (−15%). ✓
- **SPIRE real10k on** went through `CandidateBatch` (157,548
  flushes, 1,551,640 candidates) but **zero LUT32 flushes**.
  Average 9.85 candidates per flush — below the 32 threshold.
  Diagnosed correctly: the per-V2-leaf-segment scoring shape does
  not produce wide-enough batches. Packet 021 addresses this.
- **HNSW real10k on** counters all zero — the instrumented surface
  didn't exercise the `CandidateBatch` route at all. Not "HNSW too
  small for LUT32" — "HNSW didn't reach the entry point on this
  surface/profile." Don't count this as Phase 7 evidence for HNSW;
  flag for follow-up investigation (see packet 021 feedback).

## On the apparent scoring share

IVF on: elapsed_ms=2302.5 across the latency run; per-query
end-to-end p50=16.7 ms. If the 2302.5 ms covers 200 iter × 100
queries = 20,000 queries, that's ~0.115 ms scoring per query against
16.7 ms total — **~0.7% scoring share.** A kernel that's 0.7% of
query time can't move end-to-end by 15% on its own. Either:

- the counter scope is narrower than "all scoring" (e.g., only the
  block kernel inner loop, not per-flush setup),
- the 15% end-to-end win comes from cache/prefetch side effects of
  the routing change, not the kernel itself, or
- iteration count math is different (single sweep × queries).

Coder should verify the elapsed_ms span before claiming Phase 7
≥2× scoring-share. If scoring is genuinely <1% of query time on
IVF, the kernel can't meet the ≥2× *end-to-end* implication of the
gate, and the gate language needs to flip to "≥2× scoring-share
where scoring is measured directly, with explicit disclosure that
end-to-end share is small" rather than implying end-to-end wins.
