# Task 179 packet 011 — build_epoch coordinator (first outside review)

**Status: two P2s to fix (plus the cross-packet privilege P1).**

Verified good: one-transaction build-to-Ready with atomic candidate +
Ready per FR-078:326-333; the candidate persists the full 17-column
identity with `from_components` cross-consistency checks; snapshot
widening correctly mirrors `FullTransactionIdFromAllowableAt`
(epoch decrement iff xid > next-lo), canonical strictly-ascending

- **P1 (cross-packet, packet 012 P1-2):** `ec_distann_build_epoch` is
  missing the SECURITY DEFINER/search_path/REVOKE entry.
- **P2-1 — session-lock/recapture contract not enforced**
  (`build_coordinator.rs:1189-1231`). FR-078:277-279/350-352/362-364:
  build_epoch SHALL require the held-or-safely-reacquired build-specific
  session lock, and a replacement backend may not recapture the source
  under the old build id from a new snapshot. The implementation checks
  only the durable registration — any fresh backend after coordinator
  death can capture a **new** `GetActiveSnapshot` and build to Ready
  under the old build id; no session-lock ownership check, no source
  ShareLock, and the lock order is inverted (control SRE before source
  AccessShare — begin's own comment explains why that order deadlocks
  against source DDL). Damage is bounded by the durable gate, but the
  gate's residuals (RR snapshots, logical replication, the 007 P2-B
  ATTACH bypass) are exactly what this clause exists to cover. Require
  holding or begin-replay-reacquiring the session locks before capture.
- **P2-2 — competing-backend build_epoch blocks instead of
  `EC_BUILD_BUSY`.** FR-078:224-227 requires non-blocking rejection while
  ownership is live; begin implements it with `ConditionalLockRelationOid`,
  build_epoch just blocks in `open_control_index`. Fix together with the
  packet-009 P2 (shared conditional-acquire + source-first mechanics).
