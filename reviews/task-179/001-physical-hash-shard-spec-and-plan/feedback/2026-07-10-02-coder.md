## P1 findings

- **P1-1 placement hash — fixed.** FR-078 now defines version 1 as wrapping
  MurmurHash3 `fmix64(vec_id XOR 0x64697374616e6e70)`, including every shift and
  multiplier. Five hash vectors and their owner ordinals for a three-node
  roster are pinned in the specification and in
  `placement_hash_v1_golden_vectors`.
- **P1-2 manifest subrecords — fixed.** FR-082 now defines the canonical
  31-byte codec-parameters subrecord and 30-byte manifest build-options
  subrecord byte-for-byte. The latter explicitly consists of its four-byte
  version/degree prefix followed by the exact 26-byte FR-078 build-options
  body. The implementation and golden fixtures exercise those encodings.
- **P1-3 per-scan durable participant pins — redesigned.** FR-082, NFR-017,
  NFR-020, TC-042, and Task 179 now use a coordinator-local in-flight registry
  plus an exclusive retirement fence. Scans perform zero participant pin RPCs,
  catalog writes, WAL flushes, or synchronous commits. Participants never
  reclaim autonomously; normal retirement fences new local scans, observes
  zero in flight, commits a durable retire decision, and then applies
  participant reclaim idempotently. Partial apply has an explicit recovery
  operation.

## P2 findings

- **P2-1 build-gate durability/orphans — fixed.** A separately committed
  `ec_distann_begin_epoch_build` registration and mutation gate now precedes the
  first remote begin. The participant exposes
  `ec_distann_list_unpublished_generations`, so Building/Ready storage from a
  vanished coordinator is discoverable and reconcilable.
- **P2-2 publish recovery races/privileges — fixed.** Recovery and pointer swap
  are advisory-lock single-flight with a conditional catalog update. Only the
  extension owner or an explicitly granted internal role may perform side
  effects. An ordinary reader, or an authorized reader that loses the
  non-blocking lock race, continues on the prior active fingerprint.
- **P2-3 active wedged pins — eliminated by the retention redesign.** There are
  no durable participant scan tokens to wedge. Backend/coordinator death
  removes local registrations because no scan survives that process. Forced
  retirement still rejects the active fingerprint; a non-active override is
  explicit and fully audited.
- **P2-4 oversized entry — fixed.** Initial snapshot capture serializes and
  preflights the complete eventual handoff entry before graph construction or
  any remote begin. An entry that can exceed 8 MiB fails immediately as
  `EC_HANDOFF_TOO_LARGE`; v1 explicitly does not chunk entries.
- **P2-5 control-index storage — fixed.** Both topology endpoints report a
  distinct `control_index_bytes` column, and NFR-018 requires summing it into
  the graph-side numerator.
- **P2-6 logical UUID provenance — fixed.** Registration resolves the secret
  and calls the secured participant `ec_distann_control_identity` operation.
  Caller-supplied and OID-derived UUIDs are forbidden.
- **P2-7 one-owner control mode — fixed in both FR-078 and FR-079.** Every
  `distributed_control=true` roster uses the frozen AM-owned row tier,
  including one owner. Live base-table TIDs remain exclusive to the legacy
  `distributed_control=false` lane.
- **P2-8 generation terminology — fixed.** Generation now means one
  participant's physical shard; epoch means the cluster-wide roster set and
  manifest.
- **P2-9 reproducible trace audit — fixed.** The repository now contains the
  executable `scripts/audit_distann_spec_traceability.sh`. Its exact invocation
  and raw output are captured in `artifacts/traceability-audit.log`.
