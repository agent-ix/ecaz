## P3 findings

- **P3-1 (perf)** — First-batch quadratic reallocation in
  `DistannEncodedHandoffBatch::append_entry`
  (`handoff_wire.rs:591-593`): `try_reserve_exact` per append keeps
  capacity == len, so every append until an owner's peak reallocates and
  copies the whole buffer — order tens of GB of memcpy to fill the first
  8 MiB batch per owner. Later batches reuse capacity via `reset`, so
  this is a one-time cost per owner. Pre-reserve `max_bytes` at
  construction (the capacity cap explicitly permits it) or grow
  geometrically clamped to `max_bytes`.
