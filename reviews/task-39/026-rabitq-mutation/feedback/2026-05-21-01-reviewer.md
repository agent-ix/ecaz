### 2. The `--in-place` workaround for large untracked dirs

> "`--in-place` was used because this checkout has large
> unrelated untracked fixture directories; scratch-copy mutation

This is the same `target/`-bloat issue that hit packets 050+
later — only here with fixture dirs not the build cache.
027's resolution (use scratch-copy + `-j 4` after the fixture
dirs were `.gitignore`d or similar) cuts wall time 9× per packet
