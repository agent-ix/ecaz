## Findings

### 1. This is a real tooling limitation, not a verification gap

`cargo-mutants` operates on the AST and is conservative about
mutating `unsafe fn` bodies because:
- The unsafe contract may depend on specific operator semantics
- Mutating the body could violate undefined-behavior invariants
- The compiler may optimize differently around unsafe blocks

The right disposition is **document and defer**, not work
around. The packet does this correctly.

### 2. Coverage layer still applies

While mutation testing skips this file, **line coverage was
raised to 83.15%** in packet 035 (Phase-1 backing-page emulator)
and refined by 044's Phase-2 hooks. The file is *exercised* by
real round-trip tests through the emulator; it's just not
mutation-probed.

The combination of (a) real round-trip tests + (b)
mutation-tightness on the codec layer below
(`storage/{leaf_v2,leaf_v2_parts,header,helpers,...}.rs`) +
(c) emulator-level testing of the buffer/page surface gives
this file effective coverage via its callees, even if direct
mutation isn't possible.

### 3. (suggestion) Custom mutation harness is a real follow-up

The packet's reviewer-direction asks about "a custom mutation
harness (sed against flag bits inside the unsafe blocks)" or "a
future cargo-mutants release that targets `unsafe fn`."

A custom harness would be useful for:
- The `unsafe { pg_sys::PageAddItemExtended(...) }` boundary —
  bit-flag mutations on the `OffsetNumber` / `flags` args.
- Wire-bit constants inside the unsafe FFI calls.

But that's substantial new infrastructure (separate mutation
runner, separate triage format). Not in scope for this cascade.
Worth filing as a separate follow-up under task-39 or a new
hardening task if/when the rest of the burn-in is done.

### 4. Cascade closeable on this file

This file's "mutation status" is correctly recorded as "0
mutations possible under cargo-mutants 27.0.0". When the
cascade closeout is filed, this packet's outcome can be cited
as the structural reason this file is excluded from mutation
discipline.
