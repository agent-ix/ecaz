# Packet 002 — Escalation: synthetic-score-error is not citable

## Verdict change

Seq 01 marked this CONDITIONAL APPROVE as a "scoped probe."
**The verdict was too soft for an optimization task.**
Re-stating the gate explicitly: the TQ+ probe results CANNOT
be cited as motivation for any production-bound work on this
task until a real-corpus recall+timing measurement exists.

## Why this is a gating issue

Task 86 is a TurboQuant **optimization** investigation. The
only outputs that move the task forward are:

1. **Speed delta on the production scan path** at the
   canonical fixture, AND
2. **Recall delta on the same fixture** within the 0.5pp
   floor.

Score MAE/RMSE on a synthetic 1536-d anisotropic corpus
(train=192, candidates=96, queries=16) is neither. It is a
local indication that the calibration math reduces an
intermediate error metric on a tiny synthetic problem. It
does not predict either of the two acceptance numbers, and
on its own it cannot survive review as a reason to land
production code.

Specifically:
- A reduced MAE on the IP score does not imply preserved
  recall@10 — recall depends on the *ranking* induced by the
  scores, not their absolute error.
- An anisotropic 1536-d synthetic with 192 training vectors
  is not representative of the calibration's behavior on a
  10k+ real corpus where the per-coordinate distributions
  are different.

## What this packet's evidence actually justifies

Permission to **run** the real-corpus benchmark. Nothing
more. The TQ+ prototype landing in test/bench-only code
(behind `#[cfg(any(test, feature = "bench"))]`) is the
right shape for "we'll measure this." The packet's existence
is fine. What's not fine is downstream packets citing the
synthetic MAE drop as if it were evidence the prototype is
worth promoting.

Per `feedback_dont_overclaim_done`, the seq 01 phrase
"supportive of TQ+ calibration as a priority hypothesis"
overstates what the data shows. Corrected reading: "TQ+
calibration is hypothesized to help; the synthetic probe is
consistent with that hypothesis; no real-corpus evidence
exists either way."

## Required before any AM-level slice cites this packet

A side-by-side `ecaz bench suite` cell on a real fixture
(real10k+), at the IVF or SPIRE TurboQuant `no_qjl_4bit`
lane, with two columns:

| | baseline (TurboQuant) | TQ+ calibration |
|---|---|---|
| recall@10 (L=64/128/200) | row | row |

The baseline is "no TQ+", not the synthetic score-error

function.

Until both columns exist on a real fixture, this packet's
results should not appear in any options report's
recommendation chain, any AM rollout planning document, or
any closeout claim.
