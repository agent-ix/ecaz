# Task 86 — Slim-Down Decision (Option C)

Project-owner direction (2026-06-07 chat): slim Task 86 to
SPIRE LUT routing only, move TQ+ expanded validation to a
new Task 89. This addendum documents the decision and what
the coder needs to do to land it.

## Why this is the right call

The IVF TQ+ work is technically clean (real-corpus
measurement at 9 / 9 cells with recall improvement and
latency improvement), but it ships under a new on-disk
format tag (`StorageFormat::TurboQuantTqPlus = 4`) with
operator-visible reloption naming. Format commitments are
architecturally significant and hard to walk back once
operators write them into CREATE INDEX DDL.

The validation surface that exists today is narrow: one AM
(IVF), one corpus (DBPedia), one quantizer lane (no-QJL
4-bit), three scales. The win on that surface is real but
doesn't tell us what happens on:

- Other AMs (SPIRE, HNSW, DiskANN scoring patterns differ)
- Other embedding distributions (calibration generalization
  is the load-bearing question)
- Streaming-insert workloads (calibration drift unmeasured)

Shipping a format tag that locks in a tactical design choice
before that validation is done would create rollback friction
later. Slimming to the safe cross-AM-consistency SPIRE LUT
fix and deferring TQ+ to a dedicated validation task is the
honest path.
