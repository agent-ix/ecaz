# Record: keep "original-TQ in-place speedup" separate from "new-format speedup"

The closeout must not blur these two categories. They are not the same result and
only one is a validated improvement to the production scorer.

## Category A — original production TQ (4-bit no-QJL `turboquant`): small, real, validated

- Core inner scoring kernel: ATTEMPTED and could not be durably sped up.
  Packet 028 (even-dim/full-chunk fast path; shift/OR replication) and packet 033
  (prefetch) were all reverted — no stable win on the inner 4-bit loop.
- Peripheral wins, kept and validated in-engine: query-prep LUT build `-18.7%`
  (029), batch-wrapper direct-payload cascade `-3..7%` (030), QJL wrapper cascade
  `-1.8%` (031). Net in-engine TQ scorer elapsed `-5.4%` at 100k (026 -> 035).
- This is the ONLY validated improvement to the production TQ path. It optimizes
  the machinery around the scorer (LUT construction, payload feeding), not the
  inner arithmetic.

## Category B — new formats the coder invented (NOT the production format)

- New rerank formats/modes added mid-task: `turboquant2` (2-bit, pkt 008),
  `turboquant_binary` (1-bit, pkt 007), and a reverted `turboquant3`; plus a
  reduced-dimension scorer with no production format yet.
- The headline numbers — TQ2 `-86%` (032), reduced-dim `-50..-83%` (034) — are on
  these. They are faster largely BECAUSE they are smaller (fewer bits / fewer
  dims = less to compute), which is near-tautological, and they trade away the
  recall that made the original worth using. TQ2 was recall-broken (0.75-0.80,
  pkt 008) and was not re-measured post-kernel.

## Required in any closeout / summary

State the validated production-TQ speedup as exactly that: `-5.4%` in-engine
scorer-elapsed at 100k, from peripheral (non-kernel) changes. Report Category B
numbers separately and explicitly labelled as (a) a different/smaller format and
(b) unvalidated for recall. Do not present a Category-B micro-benchmark number as
"TurboQuant got faster."
