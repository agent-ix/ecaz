discriminator, at matched `nprobe=60`, TQ is faster than f32/source in **all nine
cells** (every scale × p50/p95/p99), e.g. 100k p50 `8.77` vs `9.46 ms`. That is a

blocker is the storage cost of the win, not the absence of a win. It also directly
motivates your own "future task with a changed premise (storage layout redesign)"
recommendation — that's exactly the lever that would make the existing latency edge
bankable. Worth one sentence in the closeout; not worth reopening anything.
