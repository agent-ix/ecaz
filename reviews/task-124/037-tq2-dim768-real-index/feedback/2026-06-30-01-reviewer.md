`src/am/ec_ivf/options.rs:222-240` still registers `TurboQuantBinary=7`,

## Blocker 3 — the reduced-dim rejection is invalid as a general conclusion

Packet 037 states its own method: "direct prefix-subspace scoring, not
centroid-relative residual scoring ... does not apply full-centroid IP correction
to a partial payload." That is naive first-768-dimension truncation with no
projection and no residual correction — of course recall broke. It does **not**
reject dimension reduction; it rejects a naive prefix. The status line
"reduced-dimension workload validation failed the recall contract" overstates.
Correct it to "naive 768-prefix truncation failed; a variance-preserving
projection + centroid-correct payload is untested" (that is Task 125's scope).
