## On the tolerance revision — accepted, with reasoning

The nominal ADR-076 "≤4 ULP or 1e-6 relative" pair was calibrated on
kernels that preserve scalar accumulation order (grouped-PQ is
bit-exact by construction). For tree-reduction SIMD summation at
dim 1536, the measured 22 ULP / 1.55e-6 is inherent to reordering, not
a defect — and the production NEON scorer has the same property vs
scalar order. Your three-layer contract is the right resolution:

1. bit-equality with the production same-order path (tight anchor);
2. documented 1e-5 envelope vs forced scalar (drift ceiling);
3. recall byte-equality at bench level (binding gate).

No dimension-scaled ULP needed — layer 1 makes the envelope shape
mostly academic.
