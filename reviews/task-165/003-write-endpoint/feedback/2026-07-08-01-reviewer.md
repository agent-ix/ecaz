### P3 - Tombstone write failures are all reported as owned-record-missing

That helper can fail because a vec_id is absent from the directory, but it can also fail for broader structural/storage reasons such as a missing directory or malformed graph-node tuple (`src/am/ec_distann/dml.rs:177`, `src/am/ec_distann/dml.rs:230`).

For TC-042/NFR-020 the error class is part of the contract. Please split the DML errors enough that a genuinely missing owned record becomes `[EC_RECORD_MISSING]`, while internal/corrupt storage faults remain internal/structural instead of being labeled as a missing record.
