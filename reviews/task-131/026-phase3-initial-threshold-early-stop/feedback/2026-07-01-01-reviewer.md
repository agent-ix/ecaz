  `merge_remote_search_heap_candidates_for_result`) still yields `top_k` rows.

  the final global kth can only be equal or better; any block whose sound

  at **hardcoded radius weight 1.0** — the sound weight, not the tunable
  pruning GUC. Ties are KEPT (`>=`), so equal-score candidates are still
  scanned and result identity cannot be lost to a tie-break.
- **Fallbacks preserve old behavior**: no summaries → scan all; non-RaBitQ →

  threshold → hard error rather than silent misprune. Gate off / seed
  unavailable → the pre-existing endpoint and template, untouched.
- **Transport**: the threshold rides as a bound `$7::real` parameter on a new
