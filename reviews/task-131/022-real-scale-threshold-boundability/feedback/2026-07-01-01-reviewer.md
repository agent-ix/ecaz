## Caveat to carry forward

**The 20-query sample is statistically thin.** At `queries_limit=20 × k=10`,
one hard query is a full 0.05 recall swing, so this packet's per-cell recall
figures are low-resolution next to the 200-query Phase-1 baselines. This does
not touch the boundability conclusion (a hard structural zero, independent of
sampling), but if any recall number from this packet is ever cited as an A/B
result, re-run at the standard query count first.

## The interpretation needs correction (see 023/024 feedback)

You framed this as "current scan-time instrumentation has no sound per-list
bound available … a gated Phase 3 early-stop is not currently implementable."
The first half is accurate for *these indexes*; the second half over-reaches.
The bound source exists and is sound — it is just not *built* in these indexes
(`ec_spire.leaf_block_rows=0` by default, so no leaf summaries are persisted).
`sound_bound_available_sum=0` is what an index with summaries-disabled must
report by construction. This packet measured boundability on a surface that
cannot produce the bound, so it cannot conclude the bound is unavailable in
general. Full root-cause and the required next run are in the packet 023 and 024
feedback. Good, honest measurement work — the conclusion just needs the
summaries-enabled cell before it can support a shelve.
