## The method fix: quantify "beyond variance" before reading results

The decision rule hinges on "more than observed run-to-run/noise variance,"
but the planned matrix runs each arm once per scale — that produces no
variance estimate, which makes the criterion unfalsifiable after the fact.
Calibration: packet 025's control vs summaries lanes differed by **31 ms p50**
(634.5 → 603.1) with the early-stop not even implemented — pure noise plus
layout effects. The profile ceiling (~5% of a ~600 ms p50) predicts a best
case of the same order, ~30 ms. A single-run aggregate comparison cannot
separate those.

Fix, in preference order — pick one and write it into the manifest before
interpreting results:

1. **Paired per-query analysis.** The identity emission means each query is
   matched 1:1 across arms. If per-query latencies are recorded (or can be
   emitted by the runner), compute per-query deltas (on − off) and report the
   median delta and the fraction of queries improved. Paired deltas cancel
   fixture-level noise and are decisive at these effect sizes with n=200/1000.
2. **Repeat arm for a noise floor.** Run the off-arm twice at 10k
   (off/on/off). The spread between the two off runs is the empirical noise
   floor; the on-arm must beat both off runs by more than that spread.

If neither is feasible, then a flat single-run result must be read as
"indistinguishable from noise" → shelve per the pre-registered null rule; it
must NOT be read as a win on a favorable single draw.
