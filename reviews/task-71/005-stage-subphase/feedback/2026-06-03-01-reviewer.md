## What this means for "where to push next"

If the goal is to make Task 71 actually deliver multi-× on M5:

- **`train_model` (60% share)**: already maximally
  rayon-parallel in the leader per Task 69. Further wins
  require either (a) a fundamentally different training
  algorithm, or (b) cross-worker training as in Option C —
  significant rearchitecture.

- **`stage_postings` (20% share)**: leader-side, currently
  serial. Could parallelize via per-list locks or by sharding
  posting accumulation across workers. The rejected-rayon
  experiment in packet 004 shows this is **not free** — naive
  rayon over postings caused oversubscription. The right move
  here is an explicit PG-worker shape (Option B/C) where
  posting assignment happens inside workers, not a second
  rayon layer in the leader.

So the realistic ceiling on M5 with Option A is the current
~1.30× at 100k. Pushing past that needs Option B or C, which
the Phase 1 packet rejected for engineering cost reasons.
