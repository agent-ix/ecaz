## Why — and why this is now a clean Stop Condition

The probe timing line tells the story:

- `heap_ingest_us = 54.5 ms` out of ~433 ms total = **~12.5%**
  of build time is the parallel heap-scan phase.
- The remaining ~88% is leader-side work that the Task 71
  Phase 1 design (Option A) explicitly chose **not** to
  parallelise: PQ-FastScan training, centroid assignment,
  posting-list staging, posting-page flush.

Per Amdahl: even with infinite worker speedup on the parallel
phase, the upper bound is `1 / (1 - 0.125) ≈ 1.14×`. The
observed 1.13× / 1.15× / 1.21× across 10k/25k/50k is **at the
Amdahl ceiling** for Option A on these corpus sizes.

This is **exactly the seq 01 packet 001 BLOCK concern**
materialising in evidence: Option A was chosen without an
Amdahl bound, and that bound is now visibly the cap.

## Recommended closeout: Stop Condition packet 004

Per Task §"Stop Conditions":

> Stop if Phase 1 design shows the per-worker overhead is
> comparable to the heap-scan speedup at M5 corpus sizes (i.e.
> parallel build is a wash on local hardware).

This is a soft wash — not literally flat, but capped at ~21%
because the parallelizable share is small. The honest
closeout is **packet 004 invoking Task §"Stop Conditions"**
with the framing:

- Implementation is correct (pg_test passes, equivalence
  enforced, workers spawn under suite, deterministic output).
- Best observed gain on M5 is ~21% at 50k w=4, capped by the
  ~12% parallel share inherent to Option A.
- Phase 2 implementation **stays in tree** (it's correct,
  serves as foundation for Option B/C if Graviton evidence
  later shows the wash holds there too, and validates the
  parallel-build callback surface).
- Defer the multi-× win to either:
  - Option B/C on larger corpora where leader-side work
    parallelises differently, OR
  - Graviton / cloud-class hardware where the parallel share
    may dominate differently.

Do NOT revert `amcanbuildparallel = true`. The flag is honest
— the AM supports parallel build correctly. PG's planner will
make sensible choices given the modest win.
