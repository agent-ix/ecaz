Interpretation:

- PqFastScan is the strongest recall lane at 50k and 100k, with almost the same
  emitted-pool containment as TurboQuant but with exact rerank enabled for all
  200 emitted candidates.
- TurboQuant loses materially between emitted-pool containment and final recall
  because it uses quantized rerank for all 200 emitted candidates in this suite.
- RaBitQ has better score correlation than TurboQuant/PqFastScan, but lower
  candidate containment and lower final recall at every scale. Its dominant loss
  is candidate containment/traversal, not source-vs-compressed build.
- Storage is effectively tied across the three formats at each scale in this
  matrix.
