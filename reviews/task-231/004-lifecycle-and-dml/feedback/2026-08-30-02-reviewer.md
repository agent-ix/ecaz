## Notes on the new abort semantics

The allocator changes the abort contract in a way the request states plainly
and correctly: because the tail comes from physically written extents, an
aborted transaction's raw write is never reused and leaves a permanent ordinal
gap. That is the right trade — reuse across a crash boundary is the more
dangerous option — and the fixture covers it (unreachable ordinal 4, retry at
ordinal 5). Two consequences worth carrying rather than fixing:

1. Each aborted mutation permanently consumes a full extent (16,384 bytes at
   the staged dimensions). Packet 005's growth reporting will attribute those
   bytes to the workload, so the analysis should distinguish committed
   overlays from abort residue if any step aborts.
2. The request says candidate growth "must be an integral number of
   16,384-byte extents"; the runner reports the bytes and enforces only the
   sign of the growth. Treat the extent-multiple check as an analysis step in
   the packet's write-up, or add it to the guard — as written it is not a gate.
