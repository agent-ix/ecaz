## Remaining — 1 item

1. **The non-counterbalanced wide pair can force a false STOP.** The rule says
   the 100k BW16/H25 transfer pair "cannot independently produce PROMOTE"
   because it is not counterbalanced — correct — but it *can* independently
   produce STOP: "a candidate regression of both at least 5.0% and at least
   0.50 ms is STOP." Those are the same magnitudes the primary rule uses, and
   they sit well inside the position-effect envelope this program has already
   measured: Task 230's 100k controls spanned 12.40 ms and 8.67 ms purely by
   position, roughly a 30% swing on an arm that was not changing. A single
   control-first/fixed-second pair can therefore clear a 5% / 0.50 ms
   regression band on ordering alone, and the frozen rule would read that as
   STOP.

   The asymmetry needs to go one of two ways: counterbalance the wide pair as
   well (two more steps, mirroring pair B at BW16/H25), or downgrade it to
   report-only so it can neither promote nor stop. Guarding one direction of an
   uncontrolled comparison and not the other is exactly the failure mode the
   counterbalancing exists to prevent.

## Seq-01 items — resolved

1. **Decision rule frozen and specific.** Primary metric
   `physical_benchmark_latency.mean_ms`, physical `production` arm, concurrency
   1, warm 100k BW4/H100; PROMOTE only when both independent counterbalanced
   pairs beat their own control by ≥5.0% **and** ≥0.50 ms; pairs not averaged;
   no tie-break; one pass plus one failure is STOP. Cold arms are mechanism
   evidence and explicitly cannot be called a warm-path win. Prerequisite
   failures invalidate a run rather than counting as a candidate loss. This is
   the Task 230 shape and it is frozen before any number exists.
2. **Storage predicted, then to be confirmed.** The arithmetic checks out
   against the code: `code_len_for(1536, 1)` is
   `ceil(1536 * 1 / 8) + RABITQ_SCALAR_LEN` = 192 + 12 = 204 B, giving a body
   of 6,144 + 204 + 256 + 6,528 = 13,132 B, a record of 13,212 B, an 8-aligned
   stride of 13,216 B against a page payload of 8,192 − 24 − 80 = 8,088 B,
   hence `extent_blocks = 2` and 16,384 physical bytes per node — 3,172 B
   (19.36%) of padding and slack, and two buffers touched per node visit. The
   `8192 + 16384 * base_nodes` relation-size formula as a conformance check is
   the right way to make this falsifiable, and storage is now a mandatory
   reported axis at all three scales.
3. **DML overlay growth measured and gated.** Per-owner node-store bytes before
   and after the routed workload, growth per statement, cluster aggregate, and
   a `bail!` requiring zero growth for the control and positive growth for the
   candidate.
4. **Recall floors anchored.** 0.995 / 0.950 / 0.925 derived from accepted

   candidate-minus-matched-control delta below −0.001 is a defect to

5. **Integrity prerequisites are real gates.** `precheck-host` emits a
