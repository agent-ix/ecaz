## Item-by-item

1. **Telemetry has a consumer.** `FixedStrideLogicalBlocksTouched` /
   `FixedStrideLogicalBytesTouched` are recorded in `lookup_graph_nodes` under
   `distann-head-attribution-benchmark`, matching the existing
   `ExactVectorReads` pattern, and `DistannMaterializationWork::ALL` went
   `[Self; 61]` → `[Self; 63]` with labels and indices 61/62. The CLI contract
   moved with it (`DISTANN_WORK_ROWS` 62 → 64), so a future counter added
   without updating the runner fails the physical latency step rather than
   silently dropping a Packet 005 row. The `let _ = telemetry;` is now correctly
   scoped to `#[cfg(not(feature = ...))]`.
2. **Exact vectors are batch-scoped.** `fixed_exact_vectors` is cleared at the
   top of each resolve and populated by moving the vector out of the consumed
   `LookupGraphNode` (`for (vec_id, record) in records`), so the per-node
   `clone()` is gone and residency is bounded by the active batch instead of by
   every distinct node the scan has touched.
3. **Dedup is O(1) per request.** `HashSet<u64>` sized to `vec_ids.len()`,
   folded into the existing guard as `records.contains_key(vec_id) ||
   !fixed_seen.insert(*vec_id)`. The two arms now differ in storage, not in
   dedup complexity.
4. **Checksum dependence is pinned, not assumed.** Both PG18 fixtures assert
   `SHOW data_checksums = on` — `assert_heapam_sees_empty` in the store fixture
   and the first statement of
   `test_distann_fixed_stride_stage_seal_receipt_and_topology`. Since the fast
   decoder deliberately relies on PostgreSQL's whole-page checksum for
   payload-bit corruption, asserting it in the fixture is the right place for
   the guarantee to live.
5. **The packed retry guard is page-local again.** `write_packed_node` takes
   `unpublished_tail_floor` and rejects a truncating rewrite whose first
   discarded ordinal (`ordinal + 1`) precedes the floor. The fixture calls
   `write_packed_node` directly with floor `2` to bypass the outer `write_node`
   check, so the page-level defense is proven on its own rather than shadowed
   by the caller's guard.
6. **Ready evidence is prefix-exact for packed pages.** The digest now hashes
   `b"packed-prefix\0"`, the committed slot count, and exactly
   `expected_slots * node_stride_bytes` payload bytes, so slots left behind by
   an aborted attempt — which GenericXLog does not undo — cannot perturb the
   digest. The fixture proves it: it captures a clean digest, rewrites an
   unreachable later slot with a different vector, and asserts byte-identical
   digest. Multiblock pages keep the whole-page digest binding, which is
   correct because each node owns its extent and uncommitted extents are never
   walked. The envelope is still fully verified during the digest walk
   (`decode_locked_page(..., true)`), so page identity is not weakened.
