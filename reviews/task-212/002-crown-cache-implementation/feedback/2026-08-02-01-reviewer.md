## Required before this packet closes: state the width-arm confound in the manifest

The width arms do not differ from the crown arms by the pruning GUC
alone. Per `task212-crown-suite.json`, the width steps also switch the
seed variant from `head_sample_exact` / `head_seed_count=32` to
`persisted_head` / **`head_seed_count=1`** (visible in every
`crown-width` result line and in `crown_seeds_served` = 1/query vs
32/query). The strategy switch is forced by the code gate (pruning applies
only in `PersistedHead` seed mode) and `head_seed_count=1` is what makes
the complete-shard rule prune at an observable rate — legitimate as an
**activation demonstration**, and the arm is correctly labeled
`seed_set_change=true`. But the manifest's three-column
control/crown/crown-width table invites a reader to attribute the width
column's recall/latency deltas to pruning, when three variables moved at
once.

Amend `artifacts/manifest.md` to state: (a) the width arm changes seed
strategy and seed count in addition to pruning, so its deltas are not
attributable to pruning; and (b) any future promotion of width pruning
requires a single-variable A/B — `persisted_head`, same
`head_seed_count`, pruning off vs on, at a capacity where a complete
shard fits at **all** scales (10k included, i.e. 2048 not 1024). No rerun
is required now: the packet claims activation, not a pruning win, and the
recall-run means it does cite (width slower: 51.45/77.22/98.29 ms) are
reported honestly.
