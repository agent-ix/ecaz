## Overall Verdict

Do not treat the 111g-115 sequence as performance-complete yet. The opt-in
correctness pieces mostly landed safely, but two architecture issues block the
claimed performance story:

1. 111g's index-side sidecar path scans and copies the full sidecar chain per
   query while the EXPLAIN counter reports only survivor payload bytes. This
   undermines the sidecar byte-win evidence and needs a real bounded lookup path
   or honest full-chain accounting plus benchmark proof.
2. 112/113's lazy rerank driver is safe only because no skips can fire today.
   The live call site feeds approximate scores as the driver's exact-score
   closure, so the future finite-bound/k-cap activation path would violate the
   exact-score proof unless exact rescoring moves into the driver or the contract
   is redesigned.
