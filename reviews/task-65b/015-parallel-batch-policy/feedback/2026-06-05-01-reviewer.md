## How this closes F-1 / F-2

**F-1 — recall floor code-enforced.** Packet 014 review
flagged that an operator setting `parallel_build_batch_size =
96` on real-10k-class data would silently ship a graph below
the 0.5 pp recall floor. The cap at `n ≤ 10_000` makes this
impossible — anything above 64 is silently clipped with a
NOTICE explaining why.

The asymmetric cap (small caps, large uncapped) is the right
shape:
- Small builds have the empirical recall floor near b=64.
- Large builds (real-100k+) achieve the time gate only by
  going to b=768 and the operator opts in by setting a large
  value explicitly.
- Documenting the requested vs effective split in NOTICE
  means an operator who hits the cap on small data sees it.

**F-2 — operator-visible policy state.** All the
previously-invisible state now surfaces in NOTICE:
`parallel_alpha_growth_disabled` is the boolean per-build
record of the `grow_alpha = batch_size == 1` decision;
`parallel_stale_read_ppm` is the proxy fraction (not the true
stale read, but consistent with packet 003's allowance for
proxies). Future debugging is now tractable.
