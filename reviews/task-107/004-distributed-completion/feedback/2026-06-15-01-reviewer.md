## Blocking items

### B1 — Phase 3 decision packet is missing (AC7, the point of the task)

No packet makes the keep/drop/narrow call. `004/request.md` is a
benchmark-completion record, not a decision. The task exists to answer 5
product questions (lines 113–121); none are answered in writing. **The decision
is already in the data** — it just needs a `005-product-decision/` packet:

- **Local multi-disk shows no win at product scale.** RaBitQ 1m k10 c1 mean
  latency is *worse* at 2 disks than 1: `203.3 / 361.9 / 521.1 / 658.0 ms`
  (L2, np 8/16/24/32) vs the single-store reference `187.9 / 336.2 / 487.4 /
  620.9 ms`, identical recall. At 100k the multi-disk gain is small. → answers
  Q1/Q3 toward "drop or narrow local multi-disk."
- **Multinode is where SPIRE's value lives.** RaBitQ 1m distributed k10 c1 mean
  is `89.3 / 103.6 / 111.8 / 121.3 ms` — roughly 5× lower than single-node at
  np32 and far flatter across nprobe, at np64 recall 0.9510 (vs 0.9690
  single-node). TurboQuant 1m distributed: production-read total p50/p95
  `140/164 ms`, recall 0.9490. → answers Q2 toward "keep multinode."
- Q4 needs the cited HNSW/IVF 1m baselines placed next to these SPIRE numbers
  (see B3).

A negative/narrowing result is explicitly acceptable per the task; it just has
to be written and grounded in this Pareto data.

### B2 — Multi-store (2-disk) index storage is not measured (AC5)

`ecaz bench storage` does not see the per-tablespace store data for the 2-disk
cells. Compare the in-scope 1m cells:

- `phase1-rabitq-1m-l2/.../bench/storage.log`: `ec_spire` index = **168.0 KiB,
  0.2 B/row**; total reported **15.4 GiB**.
- single-store reference: `ec_spire` index = **784.8 MiB, 831.3 B/row**; total
  **16.1 GiB**.

The ~785 MiB of L2 index data living on `ecaz_spire_store_1/2` appears in
neither `indexes` (21.4 MiB = just the corpus pkey) nor `table`, so the 2-disk
total looks *smaller* than single-disk — a measurement artifact, not a real
saving. Phase 1 requires "storage size by relation/index/store." As recorded,
the 2-disk storage rows are misleading and cannot support a storage comparison.

Required: either extend the storage step to sum per-store/tablespace size (land
in `ecaz-cli` per the runner rule), or, if that is genuinely out of reach for
this campaign, **explicitly call it out as a known observability gap** in the
decision packet so no one reads `0.2 B/row` as a real result. AC5 mandates one
of those two; right now it is silently wrong.
