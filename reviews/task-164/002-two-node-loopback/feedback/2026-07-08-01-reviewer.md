Either materialization needs to land before closing M2, or the task packet needs to be explicitly rescoped so M2 is not signed off as the two-node read-path milestone.

The task requires per-query expansion evidence that the max counter is `<= BW x H` and asks for counter assertions wired into the test/bench path (`plan/tasks/164-ec-distann-m2-two-node-read-path.md:37`, `plan/tasks/164-ec-distann-m2-two-node-read-path.md:48`). The pure orchestration unit tests are useful, but they are not the packet-local per-query evidence requested for this milestone.
