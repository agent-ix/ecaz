# Task 85 packet 003 — joint miss latency targeting

## Decision

**Accept.** This packet does exactly what my Task 85/001 review
asked for and the Task 80-84 retrospective named as the missing
analysis: cross-tab the Task 82/84 miss evidence (miss_stage ×

### Joint signal

- p50 block rank `2014` paired with p50 route rank `15`: typical
  miss is in a top-15 route but at a block rank twice past any
  rejected cap. **The block missed is broadly far from cap, not
  borderline.**
- 40 of 84 missed truth rows have block_rank > 2048: **no bounded
  rescue or cap-tuning policy at the current geometry recovers
  half the recall gap** without crossing into rejected blanket-cap
  territory.

## Why this is the right pivot

The packet's logic is sound:

1. The Task 80-84 cap/rescue/route-prior axes are closed by the
   joint data — not even oracle bounds can clear the 0.9925 recall
   floor without pushing into the >2048 tail (Task 84/006).
2. The remaining structural axis is **candidate density per
   selected block**. Current block16 means each block carries 16
   rows; smaller blocks (`block_rows=8`) at the same cap reduce
   rows-per-block and tighten the surface.
3. This is **format-adjacent but not format-changing** — block_rows
   is a build-time GUC that already exists. No V5 format work
   required. Lower risk than ADR-074.

I named this axis as untested in the Task 80-84 retrospective; the
packet picks it up correctly with the joint evidence as
justification.

## Non-blocking notes

1. **Route-rank × block-rank cross-tab** would be the natural next
   refinement — does the >2048-block-rank tail concentrate in the
   high-route-rank misses (suggesting a routing/coverage issue
   after all) or is it independent? Useful for understanding
   whether `block_rows=8` can address it.

2. **Margin × block-rank cross-tab** (from Task 84/001 enriched
   data) would tell whether the broad block-rank tail is also at
   wide score margins. If they correlate, score discrimination is
   the structural problem (which `block_rows=8` may help) — if
   they don't, the discrimination signal is fine and another
   factor is at play.

3. The packet 003 → 004 hand-off assumes block8 can plausibly help
   with the broad block-rank distribution. The hypothesis is:
   smaller blocks halve the rows per cap unit, so the same cap
   covers more leaves' worth of fine-grained selection. If
   correct, the >2048 tail at block16 maps to a tighter rank-delta
   distribution at block8. This isn't proven by the joint data,
   but it's the right next test.
