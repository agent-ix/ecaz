## Why the rationale has to change before this closes

The packet closes MAT-15 because the candidate ran 2.1× slower. That number is
real, but it is an artifact of *where* the packing was implemented — inside the
owner payload SQL, the stage packet 001 had already identified as dominant —
not a property of packed payload buffers. All +45 ms of the regression sits in
`materialize_owner_payload_sql_work` (40.38 → 118.42 ms owner-summed); response
bytes are flat to 0.06% and coordinator decode moved 0.076 → 0.096 ms.

The durable reason to close the family is the ceiling. MAT-15's addressable
cost is coordinator-side decode allocation: **0.076 ms against a 40.60 ms scan,
0.19%**. That was derivable from packet 001's counters before a line was
written. Recorded that way, the family is closed permanently and correctly.
Recorded as "our implementation was slower," someone reopens it in three months
with better SQL and spends another cycle chasing 0.19%.

`plan/design/ec-distann-recall-latency-roadmap.md` should carry the ceiling
argument, not the regression number, when MAT-15 is marked closed.

the closeout record rather than being retired with MAT-15:

1. **Per-arm generation rebuild.** Each arm provisioned its own cluster and
