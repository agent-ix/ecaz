## The confound

`materialization_correctness` is set on all six cover steps and on none of the
six no-cover steps — perfectly correlated with `covering_payload_attnums`:

```
task229-100k-pair-a-no-cover-first    cover_attnums=None  materialization_correctness=None
task229-100k-pair-a-cover-second      cover_attnums=1     materialization_correctness=True
task229-100k-pair-b-cover-first       cover_attnums=1     materialization_correctness=True
task229-100k-pair-b-no-cover-second   cover_attnums=None  materialization_correctness=None
```

That flag gates the `correctness_fixture` block in
`crates/ecaz-cli/src/commands/dev/distann_multicluster.rs:2215`, which forces
`payload_note` to `STORAGE EXTERNAL` and writes roughly 12.8 KB of uncompressed
out-of-line varlena into every odd-id row — half the table — with a `DO` block
that asserts `octet_length >= 12800` and no compression. The cover arms
therefore build a materially larger source table than the no-cover arms before
a single byte of sidecar exists.

The arithmetic confirms it is the whole effect. Predicted fixture bytes are
`rows/2 × 12,800`; observed row-tier deltas are cover-arm minus no-cover-arm
`owner_row_tier_bytes` from `results.jsonl`:

| Scale | Predicted fixture | Observed row-tier delta | Overhead |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10k | 64,000,000 B | 69,148,672 B | 8.0% |
| 50k | 320,000,000 B | 345,718,784 B | 8.0% |
| 100k | 640,000,000 B | 691,412,992 B | 8.0% |

The residual is a consistent 8.0% at all three scales — TOAST index and page
overhead, exactly as expected. The row-tier DDL itself is cover-independent:
`create_generation_relations` derives its columns from
`row_tier_column_sql(schema, build_id)`, which never consults `payload_cover`,
and the only `SET STORAGE PLAIN` in the extension is on the sidecar's own
`payload` column. There is no mechanism by which declaring
`covering_payload_attnums = 1` inflates a row tier by 41.5%, and there is an
obvious mechanism by which the fixture does.

The effect is also cleanly a cover-flag effect rather than a position effect,
which is why it survived the counterbalance: both no-cover arms report
≈1.666 GB row tier and both cover arms ≈2.358 GB at 100k, regardless of whether
they ran first or second. Counterbalancing controls for run order; it cannot
control for a variable that is confounded with the treatment itself.

### What this invalidates

- **Storage.** The 28.0–28.9% total-bytes figure measures the correctness
  fixture, not the cover. Drop it as a cover cost. The sidecar relation budget
  is a clean within-arm measurement and stands: 8,462,336 B at 100k against a
  2,498,265,088 B matched no-cover total, 0.34%, well inside the 5% gate.
- **Build.** The cover arms write ~640 MB of extra TOASTed data at 100k during
  fixture setup. The reported +14.28%/+11.34% at 10k, +3.69%/+3.98% at 50k, and
  +7.41%/+8.23% at 100k plausibly include that cost. The 10k ceiling breach is
  not attributable to the cover on this evidence.
- **DML.** Replacement and delete are measured against a table where half the
  rows carry a 12.8 KB external datum in the cover arms and none do in the
  no-cover arms. Replacing such a row is a different operation. The
  +15.22%/+15.25% replacement p95 breach and the −10.98%/+23.26% delete split
  are not clean cover measurements.
