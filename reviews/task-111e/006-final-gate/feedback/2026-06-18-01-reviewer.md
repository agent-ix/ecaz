## Verdict

Concur: **close Task 111e as a gated implementation with an iterate (not
promote-to-default) recommendation.** Together with packets 002–005, the gated

## Why this is the right synthesis (cross-cutting)

111/111a/111b/111c established what works and what doesn't as a hot dense scan
payload: only page-local dense RaBitQ-1 is a clear win; larger-payload dense
scans, page-spanning groups, and zero-copy scatter were all dominated by
contiguous-copy. Task 111e stops fighting that and instead **uses the one proven
shape (page-local dense rb1) as a cheap coarse generator and spends stronger
scoring only on a bounded survivor set.** That's exactly the conclusion the
earlier packets pointed at, turned into a real mode. Good.

## What the evidence shows (and what I checked)

- **50k: strong.** width 50/nprobe 32 → 0.9940 @ 6.6 ms; width 100/nprobe 64 →
  1.0000 @ 11.6 ms. Storage stays excellent (243 B/row at 50k, 236 B/row at
  100k — same as dense rb1). The coarse + bounded-rerank thesis holds here.
- **100k nprobe 32 is the blocker, and the diagnosis is correct.** Recall
  plateaus at 0.9730 from k100 through k1000 while total-bound p50 climbs to
  ~60 ms — i.e. widening the rerank frontier **cannot** recover the missing
  neighbors, because they were never in the rb1 coarse candidate set. nprobe 64
  recovers to 0.998–1.000 but costs latency. So the limiter is **coarse-frontier
  recall**, not rerank width — exactly as the packet states. You can't rerank a
  neighbor you didn't retrieve.
- EXPLAIN exposes the stage counters (posting pages / dense postings / candidates
  scored / rerank rows / heap blocks / approx-scan / exact-rerank), which is the
  observability a promotion decision would need.

## The recommendation chains cleanly into the roadmap

"Iterate, and the next lever is frontier *quality*, not larger rerank width"
points precisely at the live sibling tasks:
- **113 (bound-aware pruning)** and **adaptive nprobe** — get more true neighbors
  into the coarse set without paying flat nprobe 64 everywhere.
- **115 (RaBitQ residual / rb2 coarse)** — a stronger coarse representation so the
  frontier contains the neighbors at nprobe 32.
So 111e doesn't dead-end; it defines the exact problem (100k frontier recall at
low nprobe) that 113/115 are built to attack. Worth stating that handoff in the
closeout.
